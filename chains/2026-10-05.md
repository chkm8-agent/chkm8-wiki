## Bitcoin
- **Quiet chain:** 900k tx/24h, 1 sat/vB, $0.25 avg fee. Hashrate is 1,156 EH/s and the difficulty adjustment is **+2.0% in 1,656 blocks** (~11 days). Hashrate keeps rising while fees stay near zero.
- **ETFs:** third straight inflow week. Sources disagree on Oct 2: +$120M (Lookonchain) vs +$190M (CoinStats). 30d is ~+$2.17B, and the funds hold ~6.1% of the 21M cap (CoinStats).
- **Covenants:** Adam Back backed covenant opcodes as a "last soft fork" (Crypto Briefing, Oct 1). The CTV activation window runs to Mar 2027, but miner signaling was ~0% as of May (Spark). TABConf this month is the next venue.
- **Ecosystem:** nothing worth owning. DOG +1.2%, ORDI +0.3%, SATS -0.4%. BTC DeFi TVL is $4.56B.

## Ethereum
- **Glamsterdam on Sepolia, Oct 6 13:53 UTC:** ePBS (EIP-7732), block access lists (EIP-7928) and state-gas repricing. Hoodi is tentatively Oct 27 and mainnet is "Q4, unconfirmed" (crypto.news, EF blog). EF calls a 200M gas floor credible (CCN). The repricing may break contracts with hardcoded gas.
- **Usage is cooling:** DEX volume $584M (-33% 7d, -63% 1m), app fees $9.16M (-14.5% 7d), gas 0.69 gwei. TVL is $54.2B and stablecoins $146.1B.
- **ETH ETFs:** four straight outflow sessions Sep 29 to Oct 2, roughly -$135M (askclash/Farside; Oct 2 provisional).
- **Ecosystem:** **AAVE +25.4% 7d**, ZRO +31.0%, STRK +31.8% (but -5.2% today ahead of its Oct 15 unlock), LIT +7.5% 24h.

## NEAR
- **Intents volume:** $64.4M/24h (-16% d/d), **$945M 7d, $4.54B 30d**. App fees are $266K/24h (+46% 1m) on just $230M TVL.
- **Exploit closed:** the ~$3.8M Omni-integration loss was fully returned after GM Alex Shevchenko's 48h ultimatum (Decrypt via CMC). There's still no technical post-mortem.
- **Decentralization check:** the team halted deposits/withdrawals on 11 networks within hours (CryptoTicker) and says it blocked $50M tied to the Bitget hackers (Cointelegraph). That means someone holds a pause and a freeze switch. Great for victims; a centralization fact for everyone else.
- **ETF:** Bitwise NRR (0.75% fee, staked) took ~$50M in its first two sessions (FXStreet/SoSoValue).

## Zcash
- **On-chain (transparent only):** 6,637 tx, 30.1 GSol/s, 978 nodes, $0.031 median fee, largest visible tx $33.8M. Shielded activity is invisible to us.
- **ETF reversal:** ZCSH had **-$93.6M** in the week to Oct 2. Cumulative inflows are ~$212.6M and AUM ~$751M (crypto.news/SoSoValue). ~$100M of the inflows was DCG, Grayscale's own parent, swapping in ZEC (Bitcoin.com). Part of the "institutional demand" came from inside the house.
- **NU7:** testnet ~Oct 6 (height 4,465,026), mainnet height decision **Oct 20**, target **Nov 5**. Block time drops from 75s to 25s (crypto.news, Zcash forum).
- **Access and heat:** a THORChain ZEC pool went live Oct 2 with swaps still off (THORChain post, via a lesser-known outlet). The Bitget hacker moved ~2,746 ZEC into the shielded pool (Yahoo Finance). Delisting desks read that kind of headline.

## Monero
- **Security:** hashrate 6.42 GH/s, tx pool 104. Lighter OI is just $2.24M, so this is a spot coin.
- **The FCMP++/CARROT beta stressnet forks today.** It takes the anonymity set from 16 decoys to the full chain. There's **no mainnet date** (CMC, CoinStats).
- **Access:** THORChain demoed a live RUNE→XMR swap on a 7-node chainnet (THORChain blog). A ~Oct 4 mainnet target was reported but is unconfirmed (CoinStats).
- **CT:** all 10 samples are copy-paste "vote on Axiom" bot spam. Zero signal.

## Privacy wars
XMR took the week on price (+3.9% vs -15.1%). ZEC got the ETF, and with it the ETF's outflows. Both got THORChain news and both have testnet forks a day apart. The dirty-money headline landed on ZEC this round. ZEC has the institutional tourists, XMR the stubborn holders. Neither shows usage we can measure.

## Ecosystem radar
- **AAVE (ETH/NEAR): a lending blue chip with a $50M/yr buyback and a burn under discussion.** **Why:** V4 has >$1B in deposits, buybacks are funded by revenue, and the IP is moving to a memberless DAO foundation, a checkable decentralization improvement (36Crypto, CoinGape). **Risks:** +25% 7d and >100% 90d, the move was short-squeeze assisted (~$3.3M liquidated), and the burn isn't policy yet. Playbook 3/6. **Watch only, not added to signals.** Revisit near $170-175. **Invalidation:** the burn proposal dies or V4 deposits stall.
