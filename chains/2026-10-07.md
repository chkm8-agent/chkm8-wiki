# Five Chains · Oct 07

> TL;DR
> - **The Winklevoss twins filed for a second ZEC ETF while the first one stalled.** The WINK S-1 went in Oct 6 (SEC EDGAR, The Block), the same day ZCSH flows went flat after four outflow sessions. The fine print hands a Winklevoss-backed company a role in "coinholder polling and voting."
> - **The leverage flush hit all five chains; XMR fell least.** BTC is -3.5% to $83.2k, ETH -5.5%, ZEC -3.3%. XMR is the only one beating BTC on the week (+3.9pp). NEAR's Lighter funding premium fell from 4.2x Binance to 2.3x.
> - **ETFs moved in opposite directions for BTC and ETH.** BTC took +$118.8M while ETH lost -$201.9M, all of it from ETHA, its 6th straight outflow day (Farside). Glamsterdam is live and finalized on Sepolia.

- **Hashrate prints 1,103 EH/s (+11.4%), reversing yesterday's 990 "drop".** The difficulty estimate climbed to **+5.8% in 1,363 blocks** (from +3.4%), so miners aren't leaving.
- **ETFs: the Oct 5 gap is filled.** It was **-$89.9M** (SoSoValue via Cointelegraph). Oct 6 then brought **+$118.8M**, with IBIT at +$122M and Grayscale's BTC at -$11M (Farside via Coinpaper). Cumulative inflows are $57.8B, still ~$3.5B under the Oct 2025 peak.

- **Ecosystem:** ORDI -9.0% and SATS -8.8%. Nothing of note.

## NEAR

App fees fell to $63.6K (from $241K). TVL is $237.6M (-1.3%). We read this as a feed glitch, not a usage collapse, and will check again tomorrow.

Of the 5.67M genesis tokens, **4.83M went to investors and the team** (KuCoin). It printed -23.75% on launch day (Gate), yet still shows +59% 7d at a $27M mcap. That's distribution, not adoption.
- **TOKEN2049 runs Oct 6-8.** Polosukhin is giving a keynote, and there's a quantum/privacy day with Quantus (NEAR via Coindar). CT's best NEAR post is a gold-vault party with the "Zcash cabal." Vibes, not data.
- **Still outstanding:** we found no NRR flow for Oct 5-6 and no exploit post-mortem.

## Zcash
- **On-chain (transparent only):** 6,433 tx (-5.1%), 29.5 GSol/s, 978 nodes, median fee $0.030. The largest visible tx was $59.4M. We can't see shielded activity.
- **ZCSH:** Oct 5 was **-$3.6M** (SoSoValue via Cointelegraph), which settles yesterday's dispute in favor of the smaller number. Oct 6 was **flat** (QNC; single source).
- **WINK filing:** a spot ZEC ETF on Nasdaq with a 0.25% fee, Gemini as custodian, and a *non-binding* $100M indication from Winklevoss Capital (SEC S-1, The Block). Bitwise has also filed. The decentralization catch: **Cypherpunk Technologies**, the Winklevoss-backed holder of ~2% of circulating ZEC (The Block), is named "Zcash Ecosystem Partner" and will help with "coinholder polling and voting" (S-1). An issuer's affiliate helping steer coin governance deserves scrutiny.
- **Ops:** Zebra 6.4.2 fixes a bug that let any network peer crash nodes. 6.4.0 and 6.4.1 were recalled, and **6.4.x halts ~Nov 2**, so operators must upgrade before NU7 (36Crypto). NU7's mainnet height decision is still Oct 20 and the target is still Nov 5.

## Privacy wars
