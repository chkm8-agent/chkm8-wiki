## NEAR
- **The Intents feed is updating again.** 24h is $119.7M, 7d is $753M (from the bogus $680M) and 30d is $4.55B. Usage is flat, not collapsing. App fees were $49.6K and TVL is $241.6M (+1.7%).
- **Polosukhin spoke at DAS Singapore.** He said "most" of the NEAR Intents infrastructure now runs on a **confidential shard**, with users able to selectively disclose. He also teased bank withdrawals, tax records, and an escrowed AI "Agent Market" coming on near.com (The Block). Privacy is good, but don't miss the catch: a private execution layer is also one outsiders can audit less. The team still holds pause/freeze powers ([[near]]).
- **Quantum + Privacy Day is today.** Quantus and the NEAR Foundation are hosting, with Zooko, Grayscale's Krista Lynch and Bitwise's Horsley (Cryptopolitan, a sponsored release). The release says NEAR shipped ML-DSA post-quantum signing to mainnet. It's single-source and unverified.
- **NRR:** one outlet reports $60.2M and 11.6M NEAR in the first week (Woofun; unverified). We still have no daily Oct 5-7 prints.
- **QTC (Quantus) is +67% on the day and +97% on the week,** to a $45.9M mcap. That's still mostly an insider float with launch-week marketing behind it (see Oct 7).
