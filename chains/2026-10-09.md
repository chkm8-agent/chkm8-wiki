## Bitcoin
- **Short-term holders capitulated into the wick.** They sent 55.6k BTC to exchanges at a loss on Thursday (analyst Taha via Cryptonomist). The bounce back to ~$82.5k keeps the $80-83k zone as the line in the sand.
- **ETFs: -$244.1M on Oct 8,** almost all from FBTC (-$197.1M), with IBIT only -$5.5M. EZBC was the lone inflow (+$4.7M). October is now **-$407.4M** (Farside via Coinpaper, Crypto Economy).
- **US government-labelled wallets are moving coins.** Arkham/Galaxy tracked ~9,261 BTC (~$770M) going to Coinbase Prime on Oct 7-8, and a Bitfinex-hack wallet moved 12,267 BTC. No one has confirmed a sale, and the Bitfinex coins sit under a restitution order (via Unbiased Headlines/Cointelegraph). That's overhang, not a sale.
- **The chain is quiet.** It carried 654k tx (-8.2%), fees are 1-3 sat/vB, and BTC DeFi TVL is $4.44B. The hashrate print is 950 EH/s. Difficulty is now est. **+4.5% in 1,070 blocks** (~Oct 16), trimmed from +5.4%.
- **Covenants:** no news. The CTV activation client is still unsignaled, and BIP-446/448 is still draft.
- **Ecosystem:** ORDI -6.9% and SATS -4.7% on the day, both down double digits on the week. Dead zone.
