# Five Chains · Oct 10

> TL;DR
> - **NEAR ran +12.9% while BTC stayed flat.** It's at $5.40, +15.0% on the week (+17.4pp vs BTC) and back to a $7.08B market cap. Intents did $255.7M/24h again, basically matching yesterday's record, and NEAR TVL rose +7.4% to $250M. The story behind it (ETF + issuance cut + Intents) is mostly last week's news being repriced.
> - **The ETF bleeding paused, except for ETH.** BTC funds took in +$21.1M on Oct 9 (IBIT +$22.4M) after -$729M over the previous two days. ETH lost -$56.1M, all from ETHA, its **9th straight outflow day, -$697.4M in total** (Farside). The week finished BTC -$678.9M and ETH -$542.2M.
> - **ZCSH had its first non-outflow day of October,** a flat $0 on Oct 9. Its week was -$30.8M (Farside via crypto.news). ZEC is $1,229 (+1.4%). XMR slipped to $525.5, right at the level analysts called the next support.

## Bitcoin
- **ETFs: +$21.1M on Oct 9,** with IBIT +$22.4M, HODL +$2.3M and FBTC -$3.6M. The week was **-$678.9M**, with FBTC and ARKB responsible for $587.5M of it. October to date is -$386.3M (Farside via CryptoWatchDesk, SatoshiNakamoto.TV). This is a pause, not a reversal.
- **US government coins: still no sale on record.** Lookonchain counts 17,733 BTC sent to Coinbase Prime over three days (via Tokenist/CryptoTicker). CoinDesk reports the 12,267 BTC from the Bitfinex-hack wallet went to *unlabeled* wallets, not an exchange. Coinbase Prime is the US Marshals' custodian, and the March 2025 executive order bars selling reserve BTC. Counts differ depending on how wallets are attributed. Treat it as overhang, not supply.
- **Chain:** 582k tx (-11%), fees of 1-2 sat/vB (about $0.40 avg) and a mempool of 27.8k. The hashrate print is 885 EH/s (-6.8%, estimator noise). The **difficulty estimate keeps shrinking: +3.0% in 936 blocks** (~Oct 16), down from +4.5% yesterday and +5.8% on Oct 7. BTC DeFi TVL is $4.42B.
- **Covenants:** nothing new.
- **Ecosystem:** ORDI +5.1% and SATS +3.7% on the day, but both are still negative on the week. DOG is +5.2% on the week. It's a dead-cat bounce in a dead zone.

## Ethereum
- **The ETF streak is at 9 days and -$697.4M** (Sep 29-Oct 9). October alone is -$635M, the worst month since late 2025 (TechFlow citing Trader T; Kriptometa). The daily prints are getting smaller (-$201.9M, then -$160.9M, -$72.5M, -$56.1M), but they're still outflows.
- **On-chain activity cooled after the flush.** DEX volume was $1.04B (7d -33.7%), with Uniswap V3 at $159M, down from yesterday's liquidation-day level. App fees were $11.41M, TVL $51.4B and stablecoins $144.9B (-0.9%). Gas is 0.07 gwei.
- **Glamsterdam:** no change. Clients are due ~Oct 16 and Hoodi forks Oct 26 at 17:42 UTC. Mainnet still has no date, and Sepolia's 200M gas is a test parameter, not the mainnet limit (TokenPost, Crypto Times).
- **Ecosystem:** WLD +10.9% (it's the orb, so you know the drill) and DRV +9.9% (+48% on the week). STRK is +73% on the week heading into Oct 15. The M-BTC "-47.6%" undoes yesterday's "+105%" glitch, so ignore both prints.

## NEAR
- **Intents: $255.7M/24h (-0.1% d/d), 7d $1.02B (from $884M), 30d $4.76B.** It's a second day at record levels, so this isn't just a one-day liquidation spike. If yesterday's figure was carried forward instead, we'll see it in tomorrow's delta. App fees were $395.6K and TVL $250.1M (+7.4%).
- **Why it's moving:** CMC's summary credits the Bitwise ETF, the issuance-cut proposal and Intents growth. It also mentions CircuitAI launching "Confidential Intents" swaps on NEAR (CMC AI, single source, **unverified**). Bitwise's Hougan is pitching NEAR as an "active business with AI upside" (coincentral, per our digest). That's the issuer marketing its own product, but the revenue figures are real: Intents is at $2.15M/30d, +114.5% m/m.
- **Decentralization note:** Bitwise both sells NRR and co-runs the chain-signatures MPC network (Bankless, 10-09). Nobody's hiding it, but it's worth tracking how many hats one issuer is wearing.
- **Ecosystem:** RHEA +38.7% ($34.7M market cap, thin), and wNEAR is tracking NEAR. BAT +16.3% appears only because CoinGecko lists it as NEAR-bridged; it isn't a NEAR story.

## Zcash
- **ZCSH: $0 on Oct 9,** the first October session without an outflow. The week was -$30.8M (Mon -3.6, Wed -8.5, Thu -18.7), and the last two weeks total -$108.4M (Farside via crypto.news/KuCoin). October is still ~-$86M with no inflow days, and cumulative inflows are ~$182M against a $306M peak.
- **Price:** $1,229 (+1.4%), 7d -5.8%, -61.5% from ATH. Lighter OI is $23.4M and funding +0.0096% vs Binance +0.0054%, a mild long premium.
- **On-chain (transparent only):** 6,547 tx (-20%), 29.8 GSol/s, 978 nodes and a $0.028 median fee. The largest visible tx was $101.7M, still large but under yesterday's $123M. Shielded activity isn't measured.
- **Unchanged:** NU7 is live on testnet since Oct 4 (block 4,465,026). The mainnet height decision is Oct 20 and the target is Nov 5, with 75s to 25s blocks. NU7 drops v4 transactions, so any ZEC still in Sprout becomes unspendable unless it's moved first (crypto.news).
- **CT:** Moonshot vote spam, a public DCA account and a QTC-vs-ZEC "same 21M cap" pitch. Nothing organic.

## Monero

- **Hashrate 6.39 GH/s (+3.5%),** within normal range. The tx pool is 42, with 24.6k tx since the last report.

## Privacy wars

The privacy category is $31.4B (+0.6%), led by small caps: NOCK +23% (+33% on the week) and ZANO +5.9%. One flat ETF day doesn't end a selling streak, but it does stop ZEC getting worse every day.
