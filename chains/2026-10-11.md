- **ETFs:** no Saturday print. October so far is -$388.6M over 7 sessions, 4 up and 3 down (TFTC table). FBTC (-$380M) and ARKB (-$207M) did the selling last week while IBIT ended roughly flat (+$1.0M) (CryptoETF).

## NEAR
- **Intents: $153.2M/24h (-40% d/d),** after two days around $256M. The 7d total is $1.09B and the 30d is $4.76B, flat. So the record level didn't stick, but this is still above early-October levels ($117-120M). App fees were $248K and TVL $251.7M.
- **Hayes:** NEAR has "20x potential" because it's the execution layer behind private transfers. His example: shielded ZEC → NEAR Intents → USDT on Tron inside the ZODL app (NewsBTC via MEXC/finanzen). He's talking his own book, and he says so.
- **NRR took in +$13.05M last week,** one of only two inflow assets alongside XRP (CryptoETF weekly recap, single source).
- **Security:** about $3.55M of *unclaimed* NEAR-bridge assets (Rainbow/Agglayer) moved through two EIP-7702 delegations from a Tornado-funded address. The signatures were valid, but it's unclear whether the owner meant to do it (The Defiant via CMC). It's not a core-protocol bug.
- **PQ signatures:** CMC says ML-DSA is on *testnet* with mainnet "in coming weeks". That conflicts with earlier claims that it was already live (sponsored release, 10-08). We'll treat it as **unverified** until the release notes confirm it.
- **Decentralization:** the Q4 roadmap grows the chain-signatures MPC network to 21 operators (NEAR roadmap via CMC). That's the right direction given Bitwise's dual role.

## Zcash
- **$1,228 (-0.3%),** rangebound. Last week's ZCSH outflow was -$30.7M (CryptoETF), and the next print is Monday.
- **PQ roadmap:** the Zakura team is targeting post-quantum signature opcodes in **January**, with no activation height set yet. They'd cover *transparent* outputs, which hold ~70% of issued ZEC. Shielded funds need separate safeguards (CoinDesk). Zakura also has a private balance-lookup tool in the experimental Vizor wallet.
- **On-chain (transparent only):** 6,371 tx (-2.7%), 30.6 GSol/s, 978 nodes, $0.028 median fee. The largest visible tx was $23.2M, down from $101.7M, so the whale shuffling has calmed. We don't measure shielded activity.
- **NU7:** the mainnet height decision is Oct 20 and the target is Nov 5. It also changes where fees go: 40% to miners and 60% to a reserve for future rewards (CoinDesk). Sprout funds must move before the upgrade.

- **FCMP++:** stressnet v3 is still running, with no results post and no mainnet date. Our searches found no new delistings or regulatory actions.
- **CT:** "coins arrive like banknotes, no readable record of where they were last week" (24♥). The rest is Moonshot vote spam.

## Privacy wars
Hayes reopened the fight. He's bought $1M+ of ZEC since Naval told him Monero's 16-member rings are weaker than assumed and pointed to a Japanese deanonymization case (NewsBTC). XMR CT will rightly point out that FCMP++ exists to replace those rings with full-chain proofs. It's still on stressnet, though. Price-wise XMR is winning the week (-3.5% vs ZEC -7.4%) while ZEC gets the celebrity bids and the ETF redemptions. NOCK (+41% on the week, $63M market cap) is leading the small privacy coins.
