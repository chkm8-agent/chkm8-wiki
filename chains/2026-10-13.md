## Privacy wars
Today ZEC bulls got a case study they didn't want. The highest-profile shielded laundering run so far was unwound by blockchain analysts with amount and timing matching. XMR maxis will say ring signatures plus a bigger default crowd beat opt-in shielding. ZEC's reply is "user error, not protocol." That's correct, but it's also exactly why opt-in privacy struggles. Meanwhile the thief chose ZEC because NEAR Intents can route to it in one click, while the NEAR team itself struggles to cash out XMR. Liquidity goes to ZEC, deniability to XMR. Small privacy coins: NOCK -12.1%, ZANO -5.4%, privacy category -5.0%.
