# Five Chains · Oct 16

> TL;DR
> - **Relief day, but the ETFs didn't join it.** BTC ETFs managed only **+$5.1M** on Oct 15 (IBIT +$16.8M; Farside), and ETH ETFs lost **-$60.2M** for a second straight day (ETHA -$44.4M; SoSoValue via CoinScreamer). Bitcoin difficulty just went up **+5.92%** at block 971,712 (mempool.space), the biggest increase since June, so miners now earn less per machine.
> - **NEAR +11.8% while Intents volume fell -31.5%.** Price and usage went opposite ways for the first time this week. Binance futures OI *fell* ~10% during the rally (Blockchain.news), so this was short covering more than new buying. NRR +$1.13M (SoSoValue), and its holders are now in profit (~+6%, CryptoETF).
> - **ZEC +10.7% on the same day ZCSH lost -$14.8M** (SoSoValue via PANews). SoSoValue's cumulative total also settles yesterday's dispute: it only adds up if Oct 14 was **+$3.7M**, so Farside was right. NU7's mainnet height is still "TBD (to be set on OCT 20)" (ZIP 259).

## Bitcoin
- **ETFs:** Oct 15 **+$5.1M**. IBIT +$16.8M was offset by Grayscale's mini BTC -$7.6M and BTCO -$4.1M (Farside). That barely dents Wednesday's -$261.4M. TFTC has October at **-$539.8M** month to date.
- **Difficulty +5.92%** to 140.58T at block 971,712 (mempool.space API). It's the steepest increase since Jun 27 (+7.15%; CryptoTicker), and hashprice drops ~5% with it. Fees are ~0.65% of miner revenue (CryptoTicker), so miners have nothing to cushion it. The next estimate is +2.3% in 2,008 blocks (~Oct 30).
- **Chain:** 617k tx (-29.1%), mempool 69k, fees still 1 sat/vB ($0.41 avg). Our hashrate reading of 938 EH/s (-18.4%) is a noisy sample. CryptoTicker shows mempool.space at 1,032 EH/s vs blockchain.com at 587 on the same day, so don't read anything into a single print. BTC DeFi TVL $4.40B (+2.2%).
- **Price:** it held the low at ~$79.8k. CryptoTicker sees the picture intact "as long as $79,806 holds." Monthly options expire today.
- **Elsewhere:** Thailand's BTC/ETH ETF rules take effect today (The Block). CoinShares expects modest early inflows (Cointelegraph).
- **Ecosystem:** SATS +3.8% and ORDI +3.0% bounced, DOG -1.5%. All four are still red on the week.
