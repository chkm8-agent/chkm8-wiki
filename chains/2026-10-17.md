# Five Chains · Oct 17

> TL;DR
> - **The bounce is over and the ETFs are still selling.** On Oct 16 BTC ETFs lost **-$82.4M** (IBIT -$71.8M; Farside) and ETH ETFs lost **-$69.7M**, their third outflow day in a row, all of it from ETHA (SoSoValue via TokenPost). The weekly total is -$228M for BTC and -$320M for ETH (TokenPost). That's less than half the previous week's bleed, but it's still a bleed.
> - **NRR is the only ETF in our five that keeps taking money.** It took in **+$2.6M** Oct 16, for 12 inflow days and zero outflow days since launch (CryptoETF). ZCSH lost another -$1.2M (Farside), which puts it at **-$105M** for October (ZecZcash). Meanwhile NEAR Intents volume slid again, to $88M.
> - **XMR is the best of the five on the week (+7.1pp vs BTC), and its hashrate is back.** It jumped +10% to 6.17 GH/s, ending a five-day decline. ZEC is now the weakest (-3.4pp) heading into Monday's NU7 mainnet-height decision.

## Privacy wars
Over 7 days, XMR is +5.5% and ZEC is -5.0%. The coin with no ETF, getting delisted on CEXs and stuck behind KuCoin's "maintenance" is beating the coin with a Grayscale wrapper, a Winklevoss filing and NEAR Intents distribution. ZEC's institutional plumbing is working in reverse: about $105M has left ZCSH in October, and a DCG-affiliated miner is selling borrowed coins to buy rigs. The privacy category is at $30.8B (-2.8%), and NOCK (-12.8%) is the weakest small cap. Monday's NU7 decision is the one catalyst ZEC has before Nov 5.
