- **Chain:** 615k tx (-17.5%), mempool 35.9k, fees still 1 sat/vB ($0.28 avg). The hashrate reads 930 EH/s (-11.9%), but this is the same noisy sampler that printed 1,056 yesterday. What actually changed is the estimate for the next difficulty adjustment: it flipped to **-0.8%** (1,724 blocks out), from +2.0% yesterday. BTC DeFi TVL is $4.39B (+0.7%).
- **Covenants:** nothing new. CTV (BIP-119) and OP_CAT (BIP-347) are still at the draft/signaling stage, and neither is on mainnet (Crypto Briefing, Oct 1). Bitcoin is a fee-less chain that keeps arguing about how to add features.
- **Ecosystem:** ORDI -5.1%, SATS -4.4%, DOG -1.5%. All are down 8-15% on the week. There's nothing to see here.

## NEAR

- **ETF:** NRR **+$2.61M** Friday. Cumulative inflows are $83.05M and net assets $82.36M (SoSoValue via EconoTimes). The week is about +$11.7M ($9.04M through Thursday per Mitrade, plus Friday), its third straight inflow week. Average entry is ~$5.02 (CryptoETF), so at $4.77 the ETF holders are now **~5% underwater**.

- **CT:** the takes are "lower high on the 4H, needs to hold $4.50," plus a short call from $4.94. Van de Poppe has $4.80 as the level to hold (CoinCentral), and we're below it.

## Privacy wars
The spread between the two widened to **20pp in a week** (XMR +10.8%, ZEC -9.5%), and the privacy category is $30.3B (-1.4%). Every small privacy coin is red on the week too (NOCK -28.9%, XVG -13.6%), so this isn't a privacy rotation. Money is going into XMR specifically. ZEC has an ETF that keeps losing money, a DCG-affiliated miner selling borrowed coins, and a hard fork date to be set Monday. XMR has fewer exchange listings every month and is still beating ZEC. If NU7 gets a firm height Monday, that's ZEC's first real catalyst against this run. If the date slips, the "compliant privacy" pitch has nothing new to point to.
