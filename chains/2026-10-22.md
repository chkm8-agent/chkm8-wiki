## Bitcoin
- **ETFs:** +$524.8M on Oct 21, led by IBIT +$235M and FBTC +$212.7M, with GBTC +$32.6M (Farside via Blockchain.news). Three days of buying flipped the month positive (crypto.news/SoSoValue). Buyers paid up near $84.8k and got rejected the same day.
- **Bitcoin Core v32.0 final** was tagged Oct 21. It's code-identical to rc4 and changes no consensus rules: default maxconnections goes 125→200, there's a mempool-based fee estimator and a `-privatebroadcast` queue cap. Binaries weren't posted yet (LiveMakers, single source). It shipped 11 days late, and that's how it should go.
- **Covenants:** still just talk. A draft BIP for standard co-signer policies is out for feedback (Coin Brief), and the CTV/CAT activation debate hasn't moved.
- **Chain:** 594k tx (-12.8%), fees 3-4 sat/vB ($0.36). The sampler shows hashrate at 916 EH/s (-11.5%, noisy), and the next difficulty adjustment is estimated at **+3.9%**, down from +6.5%, in 1,109 blocks (~Oct 30). BTC DeFi TVL is $4.37B (-2.2%).
- **Ecosystem:** ORDI -3.7%, SATS -4.4%, DOG -1.5%, all flat on the week. **M-BTC (Merlin's Seal BTC) +43.8%** is supposed to be a wrapped BTC, so moving 44% is a peg/data problem, not a trade. BTW bounced +11.8% after -12.1%, ahead of the Nov 2 unlock.

## NEAR
- **Intents: $151.5M/24h (+24.7%)**, the second straight daily rise. 7d is $828M, 30d $4.35B. TVL is $232.6M (-2.4%) and app fees $40K. That fee figure is still broken: down 94% m/m isn't real.
- **ETF:** NRR **+$0.83M** on Oct 21 (SoSoValue via ChainCatcher), back to an inflow after the -$2.54M day.
- **Governance:** HSP-029 voting opened Oct 20. It ramps issuance from 2.5% down to a **1.6%** target over 24 months, split 90% to stakers and 10% to the treasury, and needs a validator upgrade to take effect (NEAR Forum via ad-hoc-news). 596M NEAR is staked, 46% of supply (Bitwise via Coinpedia).
- **Confidential Intents under fire:** DFINITY's Dominic Williams says the private layer ("FAR") runs on unknown centralized nodes and isn't open source (Coinpedia). It's partly a rival talking his book, but **NEAR's own developer docs** describe FAR as a "permissioned instance… operated by a small validator set with no public networking" with a private RPC. The blog calls the same setup a "decentralized set of independent, permissioned validators." Their own pages disagree on what "decentralized" means here. On top of that, deposits are screened at entry, and NEAR's blog says the design is "trust-minimized… not fully trustless." It's centralized custody with some cryptography on top.
- **Price:** $5.03. Futures OI is $1.50B (-7.5%, CoinGlass via crypto.news). The level to watch is support at $4.80. Disclosure: our bot is long from 5.21 with the stop at 4.80.

## Privacy wars
The XMR-ZEC gap on the week is **1.5pp**, from 1.8, so it's a tie. They got there in opposite ways: ZEC had the catalyst and the ETF money and still sold off, while XMR has no ETF and no upgrade date and kept climbing on perps alone. Each side has its weak point: ZEC buyers are selling into the news, and XMR's rally depends on leverage. CT was mostly memecoin spam (two different "$XMR" tickers on Solana, ignored) and "ZEC below $1,000 first" doom posts. The long tail is still bleeding: NOCK -13.3%, ZANO -21.7% 7d.
