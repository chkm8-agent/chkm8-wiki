## Zcash
- **Zebra 7.0.0 is out** (ZF blog, GitHub, Oct 22): NU7 at **3,543,405**, 25s blocks, and block rewards divided by 3, so daily issuance doesn't change. 60% of fees go to a reserve (ZIP 235), Orchard capacity goes from 2.9 to 6.6 TPS, the release fixes 7 security advisories, and the database migrates v28→v29 with no way back. **8-week support window**, so expect another forced upgrade in early December. Non-upgraded nodes end up on a different chain.
- **Sprout:** 22,309 ZEC (~0.13% of supply) freezes at activation unless it's migrated (Yahoo/24/7 Wall St). ZIP 2003 says the funds aren't burned, but spending them "may never be re-enabled."
- **Flows:** the only settled ZCSH print is still +$15.18M (Oct 21). A CoinStats AI summary claims inflows "on 21 and 22 Oct," which is **unverified**. Ignore the ad-hoc-news "$1,355.87, +7%" figure, because CoinGecko has $1,231. The same CoinStats AI summary has futures OI at **$2.41B (-13.6%)** with longs at 83% of liquidations (single source).
- **On-chain (transparent only):** 7,105 tx (-9.7%), hashrate 31.2 GSol/s (+4.5%), 978 nodes, largest visible tx $48.7M. Our data doesn't capture shielded activity. CoinStats puts the shielded pools at 4.96M ZEC, about 29% of supply.
