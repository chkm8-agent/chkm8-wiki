## NEAR
- **Intents: $132.3M/24h, flat.** 7d is $772M (from $861M) and 30d $4.04B. Usage held while price fell 10%. TVL is $209M (-9.4%), mostly because NEAR itself got cheaper.
- **The IP fight:** Williams' claim that NEAR runs on "8 servers" is what moved the price (dmarketforces). NEAR's technical post, published half an hour before Williams' piece, says mainnet MPC runs **17 independent operators, 11-of-17, inside TEEs** (cryptonews.net). Haseeb Qureshi points out that the DFINITY patent was granted only in **late July**, NEAR swapped the algorithm in September (PR #4503), and the Groth-Shoup paper is CC BY and is cited by NEAR (TechFlow). If that's right, any infringement covers about a month. No lawsuit has been filed, and NEAR Foundation still hasn't formally responded. **The decentralization point is still fair.** 17 TEE operators is better than 8, but it's still a small, permissioned set, and NEAR's marketing doesn't say that.
