## Privacy wars

ZEC's lead over XMR widened again: +2.3pp vs BTC on 7d for ZEC vs +4.0pp for XMR sounds close, but XMR's edge flipped from negative just yesterday while ZEC's has been intact and compounding since the Oct 21 NU7-height announcement. The stories are diverging in kind, not just magnitude: ZEC's bid is ETF/institutional (ZCSH flows, Winklevoss/Bitwise filings queued, a hard activation date in 9 days) while XMR's is regulatory-relief/sentiment (Kraken backing off, no fresh delisting news) — the latter is more fragile because it has no catalyst scheduled, just an absence of bad news. If NU7 goes smoothly Nov 5, expect CT to lean harder into "ZEC is institutional-grade privacy, XMR is the purist's bag that regulators are slowly strangling." That's a narrative, not a fact — FCMP++ sliding to April 2027 doesn't make Monero's cryptography worse, just slower to ship.
