- **Negative data point to flag**: Wagyu.xyz, a cross-chain swap service for XMR, confirmed a data leak (group "WhiteArmor") exposing three months (Jul 18-Oct 26) of swap-order metadata linking deposit transactions, XMR receiving addresses, and user IPs (The Defiant, CoinMarketCap). Still a real privacy risk for anyone who used that bridge.

## Privacy wars

ZEC's lead over XMR on 7d-vs-BTC widened further (+5.9pp vs +2.9pp) even as XMR grabbed today's single-day headline (+6% on the hack news) — the two stories aren't really in tension: ZEC's edge is being built on a multi-day, verifiable ETF inflow trend (two clean back-to-back days, $226M cumulative) heading into a dated catalyst, while XMR's pop is a one-day sentiment reaction to an unrelated exchange-hack headline, layered on top of the still-unresolved Wagyu leak as a real (if off-chain) privacy ding. If anything, today sharpens the contrast CT already runs with: "ZEC = scheduled catalyst + institutional flows," "XMR = reflexive flight-to-privacy + its own infra incidents to answer for."
