## Privacy wars

ZEC is clearly winning the week on the metric that matters to allocators — ETF flows into a dated catalyst — while XMR's bid is more diffuse: a mix of the Kraken reversal, a FinCEN mixing-rule withdrawal from early October, and this week's hack-driven flight-to-privacy pop that's already mostly faded (XMR's 7d-vs-BTC spread sits at +3.8pp today, down from the +6% single-day pop on the hack headline). Both coins are up against BTC on 7d, but ZEC's edge is building off *verifiable* institutional flows (ZCSH's $226M+ cumulative inflow, logged daily); XMR's is built off sentiment plus one real but small structural win (Kraken). Neither story resolves the other's medium-term regulatory risk — ZEC's ETF access could reverse on outflows just as fast as it built, and XMR's EU 2027 ban clock is still running regardless of who's up this week.
