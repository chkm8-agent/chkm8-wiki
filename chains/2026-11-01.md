## Privacy wars

Yesterday's framing — ZEC pulling away on a verifiable ETF catalyst while XMR's bid was diffuse sentiment — got a reality check today from both sides. ZEC's edge nearly tripled in reverse (+15.0pp → +6.1pp) the moment its ETF inflow streak broke, confirming that edge really was flow-dependent, not structural. XMR's story got worse on the regulatory axis specifically: Kraken shutting EEA access is the kind of access loss ZEC's ETF is designed to avoid, and it's a sharper, more concrete risk than the diffuse "EU ban coming in 2027" framing we've used before — it's happening now, piecemeal, exchange by exchange. Neither coin's medium-term access risk improved today.
