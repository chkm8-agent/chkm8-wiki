- **Correcting yesterday's item:** we reported Kraken cutting off Monero trading/deposits for the entire EEA on Oct 31, 2026. Re-checking the primary source (startupfortune.com) today, its own body text says that cutoff happened "in October 2024" — and Kraken's own support article confirms the only regional XMR delisting on record is Ireland & Belgium, effective June 10, 2024 (the page itself was merely "last updated October 19, 2026," which is likely what triggered the misread). There's no evidence of a new 2026 EEA-wide Monero delisting. - The one real data point in yesterday's source still stands on its own merits: TRM Labs (cited by startupfortune) found ~48% of new darknet markets launched in 2025 accept only XMR — worth treating as a single-citation, unverified-by-us figure, but it doesn't depend on the EEA-delisting error.
