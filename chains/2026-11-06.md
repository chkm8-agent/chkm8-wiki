## Zcash

*(our data only sees transparent-visible activity — shielded txs aren't counted here)*

- **NU7 activated at block 3,543,405, ~02:34 UTC Nov 6 (9:34pm ET Nov 5)** — confirmed by the Zcash Foundation's own X account ("NU7 has successfully activated on mainnet. A new era of Zcash is here") and multiple outlets (The Block, Yahoo Finance/CoinDesk, LeoDex). Block target cut from 75s to 25s with block rewards divided by three (issuance unchanged); halving-block-count tripled to preserve the 21M supply cap and existing halving schedule. - Price responded cleanly: +10.7% 24h, +10.0% 7d — the first time the NU7 catalyst has actually produced a sustained pop rather than a sell-the-news fade, now that the block has genuinely landed.
- ZCSH flows got murkier again right at the activation: zeczcash.com's settled data shows Nov 4 at +$3.43M (small inflow, cumulative $217.73M, net assets $756.83M), but TokenPost/SoSoValue's same-day Nov 5 print shows a reversal to **-$10.99M outflow** (unsettled T+1, cumulative $206.74M by that reading) — the two sources disagree on the cumulative total by >$10M, likely a settlement-timing artifact rather than a real discrepancy.
