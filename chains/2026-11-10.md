## Monero
- Hashrate 6.63 GH/s (-3.4% d/d), tx pool 89, height 3,803,401, ~31,343 new tx since last report.
- New regulatory wrinkle, sharper than the usual exchange-delisting pattern: South Korean police adopted a new national custody standard for seized crypto (multi-sig cold wallets, full loss compensation) that **explicitly excludes privacy assets like Monero**, requiring them to be isolated in separate hardware wallets rather than the standard custody flow (CoinMarketCap AI, Nov 9). It's a custody carve-out by a government body, not a private exchange's listing call — a different, arguably more durable kind of institutional exclusion than Binance/Kraken delistings. No change to the EU AMLR July 2027 full-ban timeline or FCMP++'s non-binding spring 2027 mainnet target.
- Price dropped -7.1% 24h, the worst mover of the five today, after roughly doubling since July; no XMR-specific catalyst found for the drop — reads as profit-taking/risk-off alongside the broader privacy-sector pullback (ZEC also down -3.3%).
