## NEAR
- Intents volume 24h $152.75M (-8.1% d/d), 7d $937.97M, 30d $3.76B. App fees $316.88K (1m +53.1%), TVL $174.82M (+2.1%), stablecoins $131.32M.
- **NEAR AI (the ecosystem's AI product arm, a separate trust boundary from NEAR Protocol itself) disclosed a security breach**: an attacker exploited a remote-code-execution bug in the open-source SGLang inference engine to reach a deprecated "Private Chat" feature, pulling account details (emails, names, plan/balance info) for ~100 users and accessing chat history/settings/logs for 18 legacy agents; two Cloud customers' API keys were exposed and have been revoked (The Block, Nov 10, via NEAR AI's own incident report). NEAR AI says NEAR Protocol and its other services were unaffected, and the vulnerability is now patched across all inference servers — but it's a real reminder that chain-level security claims don't automatically extend to every "NEAR" product.
- Price +0.8% today while NEAR AI's news broke — no visible market reaction, suggesting the market reads it correctly as an app-layer, not protocol-layer, event. HSP-029's Security Council veto window (zero recorded opposition) still closes Nov 16.
