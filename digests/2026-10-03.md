**Candidates**
1. **RAY: Solana's original DEX found a second customer after Pump.fun left.** After PumpSwap took graduations, quarterly fees fell 96% (from ~$412M in Q4'24 to ~$17M in Q2'26). StonkFun (integrated Sep 5) and tokenized-stock listings brought 30d fees back to ~$41.7M (Fintrender, DefiLlama). **Value accrual:** 12% of AMM fees buy RAY daily. LaunchLab adds 25% of its protocol fee as buy-and-burn. Holder revenue was $4.43M/30d (The Merkle). Mint authority is disabled, emissions are ~1.9M RAY/yr, and there is no VC cliff. **Playbook 5/6** (usage, accrual, left-for-dead at -88% from ATH, one-liner, clean supply). The distribution catalyst is weak because Raydium doesn't own StonkFun. **Risks:** revenue swings hard and depends on launchpad and meme flow. Bought-back RAY sits in a protocol wallet (now >30% of circulating), not burned. A **3-of-5 treasury multisig with no timelock** sets the 12% share, and programs are upgradeable by a 3-of-4 multisig with a 24h timelock (Fintrender). That's decentralized-ish at best. **Buy trigger:** revenue holds through October, or the treasury stack gets burned. **Invalidation:** 30d revenue back under $3M, or a close < $1.50. Spot $2.06 (+5.4%, 30d +149%), so wait for a dip.
2. **UNI: the fee switch finally pays, and Robinhood Chain feeds it.** Since Dec 2025, the UNIfication fee switch has sent protocol fees to TokenJar, which can only buy and burn UNI (Firepit). The Jul 27 votes extended it to v4 and Robinhood Chain, and daily burn funding rose from ~$114K to ~$325K (Odaily/KuCoin). In early Sep, Robinhood Chain supplied **71.9%** of protocol revenue (The Traders Spread). **Playbook 4/6** (usage, burn, distribution partner, one-liner). It is not left for dead: it's up from the $2.39 June low to $9.15. **Risks:** one company's chain is most of the fee base, and the gas subsidy just ended. Uniswap Labs gets a 20M UNI/yr growth budget, and fee-funded burns only recently outpaced it. AMC has objected to stock tokens. **Buy trigger:** October data shows Uniswap holder revenue holding after the subsidy. **Invalidation:** Robinhood Chain's revenue share collapses, or a close < $6.50. Price $9.15.

Not advice. AERO and SKY were added 10-02 and are still open (AERO +1.3%, SKY -3.3%). Not a candidate: **ZRO** (see Movers).
