There are open-market buybacks today: 162k ZRO ($347k) on Oct 6, **2.538M ZRO / ~$5.4M cumulative** (Arkham via Gate, crypto.news). The Foundation bought 50M ZRO (5%) back from strategic partners in Sep 2025 (layerzero.network/blog/the-zro-token). **Playbook 4/6:** value accrual (live buyback, burn designed), distribution catalyst (ATLAS/Zero, reported Citadel/Google backing for Zero per Bit2Me), left for dead (ATH $7.47, now $2.20), clear one-liner. The buyback is small change next to that. Foundation/Labs run it, so decentralization is 2/3. It's already +97% in 30d (crypto.news), and futures OI is ~$279M. **Buy trigger:** holds above $1.80 after the Oct 20 unlock, with ATLAS live. **Invalidation:** close < $1.50 or an ATLAS delay into 2027. Spot $2.20. Not advice.
