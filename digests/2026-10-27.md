     drives ~65% of revenue (Cash spend ~$100M/month, per PANews). Restaking sector led today's sector table (+2.9%).
   - **Revenue:** real and non-incentive — DefiLlama shows $3.8M/30d protocol revenue, $12.2M/30d fees (Sector Alpha).
     Passes the mandatory revenue screen that QNT/GRASS/VVV failed.
   - **Value accrual:** a live buyback exists (weekly eETH-fee purchases, monthly revenue-share purchases) but
     TechFlow (Oct 18) found it's missing its own guidance by ~27%, and nearly all bought tokens get redistributed
     as cashback — users cashed out faster than the Foundation bought for the first time the week of Oct 12. This is
     closer to a customer-acquisition cost paid in ETHFI than a PUMP/UNI-style burn.
   - **Score ~3/6:** revenue ✓, accrual ½ (real but leaking), distribution catalyst ✗ (nothing disclosed today),
     rotation from dead cap ✗ (not left-for-dead), clear one-liner ✓, unlock overhang unknown.
   - **Risks:** buyback mechanism could keep leaking net-negative; no ETF or partner catalyst in today's data; TVL
     tied to ETH staking trends which saw net outflows mid-October.
   - **Would watch for:** a week where purchases > cashback outflows again, or a disclosed % revenue share increase.
     **Invalidation:** revenue turns negative m/m. Not added to signals.json (accrual too weak to clear the bar). [[ethfi]]
