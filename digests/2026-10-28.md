1. **Watch-only: ORE: "Solana's fair-launch mining token, rebuilt twice, now funds its own burn."**
   - **Narrative:** ORE launched April 2024, immediately crashed Solana with spam transactions ("ORE breaking
     Solana," -97% from peak), rebuilt with DrillX hashing in V2, then rebuilt again Oct 2025 into a 5x5 grid
     staking game. Miners stake SOL on a cell each minute; 10% of losing-cell SOL auto-buys ORE on the open
     market — 90% burned, 10% to stakers.
   - **Revenue:** $3.99M/30d, **+62.7% m/m** (DefiLlama, tagged "Gamified Mining"). A DefiLlama adapter note says
     this only captures the protocol's 10% cut — true round volume is roughly 10x higher.
   - **Value accrual:** the strongest we've screened — 99% of captured revenue routes to holders via direct
     burn+stake, with no leakage identified (contrast ETHFI's cashback leak, or GRASS's one-off buyback).
   - **Score ~4.5/6:** revenue ✓, accrual ✓✓, rotation from dead cap ✓✓ (-88.8% from ATH after two
     network-breaking restarts), clear one-liner ✓, no vesting overhang by design ✓; distribution/access catalyst
     ✗ (no ETF or mega-partner).
   - **Risks:** it's a zero-sum gambling game — most participants lose SOL each round, so revenue depends on
     continued gambling appetite, not sticky utility demand. We can't confirm today's price momentum from the
     data pull (ORE isn't in today's movers tables). CoinGecko shows a 3M max supply; other sources describe a 5M
     cap carried over from V2 — unresolved conflict worth checking before sizing anything. "Zero insiders" claims
     sit alongside a $3M VC seed round (Foundation Capital, Solana Ventures) into the team.
   - **Would watch for:** a clean current-day price/volume read confirming the revenue growth is showing up in
     price, and resolution of the supply-cap conflict. **Invalidation:** revenue turns negative m/m, or round
     volume collapses. Not added to signals.json. [[ore]]
