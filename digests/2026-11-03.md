**Candidates**
No new spot picks. Two checked and disqualified:
1. **Skipped: RUNE (THORChain), today's top gainer (+11.4% 24h).** Its recent "revenue spike" traces to North
   Korean hackers laundering ~$48M of stolen Binance-hack funds through THORChain pools on Oct 31 — a 2,229%
   one-day jump in protocol fees that is not organic usage (CoinMarketCap). The protocol has also had swaps halted
   on 10 chains since Oct 31 after an Ethereum reorg exposed a refund bug, with no restart date as of this pull, on
   top of a separate $10.8M cross-chain hack on Oct 17 (ZachXBT/Arkham). The RUNE burn allocation was recently cut
   from 5% to 1% of system income — value accrual getting *worse*, not better. Fails the real-usage, value-accrual
   and clean-story checks at once.
2. **Skipped: NIGHT (Midnight).** Cardano's new privacy L1 went fully live Oct 29 and is riding the same
   privacy-sector tailwind as ZEC, with Hoskinson predicting it'll "outgrow Zcash." But the tokenomics give NIGHT
   holders zero accrual: fees are paid in DUST, a non-transferable resource generated by holding NIGHT and burned on
   use — the burn benefits neither NIGHT nor ADA directly, there's no revenue line to point to (the chain is weeks
   old), and staking/decentralization doesn't open until Q1 2027 (enterprise-run today). Good narrative, zero
   playbook checkboxes. Watch, don't buy.
3. **Already open, not re-screened today:** ZRO, AERO, SKY, RAY, UNI, PUMP, FLUID, MET, KITE (short idea, unlock
   window Nov 1-3 closes today). KNTQ/STX not re-screened.
