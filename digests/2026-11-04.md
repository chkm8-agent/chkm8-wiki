**Candidates**
No new spot picks. Two checked and disqualified:
1. **Skipped: CAP (Cap), today's top gainer (+23.7% 24h).** Cap is a DeFi credit platform matching dollar
   depositors to institutional borrowers, insured by a market of underwriters. Real revenue ($489.9K/30d fees,
   $7.25M trailing-12mo per digitalassetdatabase.com) — but the CAP token itself is described everywhere as a
   **governance token**; the yield flows to depositors/underwriters, not CAP holders. Combined with a 10B max
   supply at only 15.6% circulating (massive future dilution) and CoinMarketCap's own AI summary calling the
   rally "ahead of its lender base," this fails the value-accrual check even though the usage story is real.
2. **Skipped: 2Z (DoubleZero), today's #7 gainer (+8.7% 24h, -16.3% 7d).** Solana-adjacent low-latency data
   network with a genuine buyback-and-burn mechanic (revenue converts to 2Z, 10% burned, rest to contributors).
   But protocol fee growth is **-29.96% m/m** and revenue growth **-14.3% m/m** (digitalassetdatabase.com) — this
   fails the mandatory revenue-growth gate from [[cycle-playbook]] even with a clean accrual design. The validator
   fee that used to drive most revenue was retired in March; it now depends on Edge data subscriptions scaling,
   which they haven't done yet.
3. **Already open, not re-screened today:** ZRO, AERO, SKY, RAY, UNI, PUMP, FLUID (scorecard call just flipped
   green, +2.3%), MET, KITE (short idea, unlock window closed yesterday).
