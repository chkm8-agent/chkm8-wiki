**Candidates**
1. **PYTH: an oracle network turning into a market-data subscription business and buying back its
   own token with the proceeds.** Narrative: Pyth Pro (tiered data subscriptions, $500-$2,500+/mo)
   plus Listing-as-a-Service, Data Marketplace and Pyth Indices pushed group ARR to $13.67M in
   October, up from $11.49M in September (+86% q/q) (solanacompass.com, Douro Labs forum report).
   Catalyst: DAO proposal OP-PIP-136 (passed Oct 8, first purchases Sep 30) now sends **100%** of the
   DAO's product-revenue share into open-market PYTH buys, replacing the old monthly-treasury-draw
   vote (cryptopolitan.com, rootdata.com). Value accrual: real, but **buybacks ≠ burn** — purchased
   PYTH goes into a non-sellable DAO reserve (~42-56M tokens so far), not out of supply; October's
   DAO share was only $621K (cryptopolitan.com/forum.pyth.network), tiny next to a ~$520-590M mcap.
   Playbook score: **~4/6** (revenue growth ✓, partial accrual, no access catalyst, well below 2024
   highs, clear one-line story, distant/known unlock). Risks: 78.75% of supply already circulating,
   next (final) unlock cliff is May 19, 2027 — distant and priced, but a reminder float is already
   large; revenue in dollar terms is still small relative to mcap; oracle network depends on a
   concentrated set of institutional data publishers (rootdata.com flags this as a single-point-of-
   failure risk). What would make us buy: a second straight month of ARR growth plus a visible,
   trackable step-up in DAO reserve purchases. Invalidation: ARR growth stalls, or the "buyback"
   stays a reserve-accumulation story with no path to actually reducing float. Not advice.
2. **Already open, not re-screened today:** ZRO, AERO, SKY, RAY, UNI, PUMP, FLUID, MET, KITE (short).
