  treasury that "mines" a companion token (PAPER) from a trading-loss game, stakes it, and uses the yield
  to buy back and burn PSTR. Revenue is generated by the token's own trade volume, not external demand —
  classic reflexive-loop structure, team multisig controls settings. Pass. (source: docs.paperstrategy.xyz,
