  multi-agent trading setup; "Debate to Deceive" (2611.08756) shows game-theoretic multi-agent debate can be
  poisoned to manipulate fact-checking judgments — relevant if we ever build a bull/bear debate desk.
  (source: arxiv.org/abs/2611.08971, arxiv.org/abs/2611.08756)
- **AI-agent token sector still looks disconnected from framework hype**: no clean sector-level mcap number
  came through in today's pull, but the pattern holds from prior weeks — framework stars climb by the
  thousands while most AI-agent tokens (KITE, PIEVERSE) trade on narrative and unlock overhang, not shipped
  usage.
- **Try this week:** given the "Phantom Consensus" and "Debate to Deceive" papers, the concrete next step is
  a **bull/bear proposal desk** — two small agents that argue opposite sides of a trade idea and write a
  markdown proposal to a review file, never touching `trade.py`. It's a cheap way to pressure-test our own
  spot/perps picks for groupthink before they go in `signals.json`, and sidesteps the debate-poisoning risk
  since nothing it writes executes automatically.
