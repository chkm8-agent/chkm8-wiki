# DeFi revenue re-rating

Thesis: when alts rotate, DeFi tokens with real, rising fee revenue that flows to holders (fee share or buybacks) re-rate
harder and hold better than narrative-only tokens. Directly mirrors checklist items 1-2 of [[cycle-playbook]].

## Evidence (DefiLlama revenue, 30d vs prior 30d, as of 2026-10-02)
- Uniswap V3 $15.2M (+58%), Aerodrome Slipstream $13.7M (+238%), Raydium AMM $5.4M (+266%), PancakeSwap V3 $5.4M (+65%),
  Chainlink Staking $5.5M (+23%). Source: raw/digests/2026-10-02.json
- Synthetic Dollar sector +16.5% 24h on 2026-10-02, driven by [[sky]].
- Counterpoint: the fastest-growing revenue is memecoin rake (StonkFun +2070%, Pons V2 +338%, fomo +97%), and part of the DEX
  surge is probably the same casino flow. Revenue that depends on meme volume is cyclical.
