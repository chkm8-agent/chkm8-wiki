# AERO (Aerodrome → Aero)

Base's dominant ve(3,3) DEX, built by Dromos Labs (also behind Velodrome on Optimism). Narrative: [[defi-revenue]].
Screened via [[cycle-playbook]] on 2026-10-02 (5/6).

## Narrative
"The unified liquidity layer for Ethereum." Aerodrome and Velodrome merge into one token/exchange, **Aero**, on MetaDEX03,
across Base, Ethereum, OP, Arc, Ink, Robinhood Chain and Arbitrum. Per TokenPost (2026-09-29), launch is **Oct 21**.
Swap ratio is set by trailing 52-week revenue: ~94.5% to AERO holders, ~5.5% to VELO holders.
Sources: https://www.tokenpost.com/news/business/24354, https://aerodrome.finance/docs, https://dev.to/claudia-ve/the-aero-merger-is-back-on-track-audits-nearly-done-september-launch-window-and-why-its-bullish-226l

## Catalysts
- Oct 21 merger launch. The date already slipped from Q2 2026 to July to September, so delivery risk is real.
- Distribution: Robinhood Chain listed as a venue, the same distribution angle that helped [[lit]].
- DefiLlama: Aerodrome Slipstream revenue $13.73M/30d, +238% m/m (raw/digests/2026-10-02.json).

## Value accrual
100% of exchange revenue goes to veAERO lockers weekly as voting rewards (fee share, not buyback/burn). Lifetime exchange
revenue (Aerodrome + Velodrome) is $568M. Quarterly revenue went $111M (Q4'24) → $16.4M (Q2'26) → $21.0M+ (Q3'26, in progress), so it's recovering
from a deep trough. Source: https://aero.xyz/economics/metrics/

## Unlocks / insiders
No cliff unlock found, but there is continuous dilution: AERO emissions run ~12.7% annualized (weekly ~4.8M, of which ~220K
goes to the team). The team allocation was 95M veAERO auto-max-locked at genesis. Lock rate ~50%. Source: aero.xyz metrics, aerodrome docs.

## Decentralization
Score **2** (provisional). The Aerodrome Foundation "determined" to hold emissions at ~10.92%/yr instead of handing the
decision to token operators as originally intended, and one company (Dromos Labs) runs both DEXes and the merger.
Gauge voting is real onchain governance of emissions *allocation*. Source: https://aerodrome.finance/docs
