# Arbitrum (ARB)

Largest Ethereum optimistic rollup; Arbitrum Orbit/Expansion Program licenses the stack to other chains, most notably
Robinhood Chain. Related: [[l2-shutdowns]] (the inverse story), [[uni]] / [[defi-revenue]] (Robinhood Chain dependency), screened against [[cycle-playbook]].

## Narrative
"Robinhood's landlord": an L2 that sells blockspace/licences to big brands as an infrastructure business. Foundation met 40+
European allocators (~€800B client assets) on 2026-10-17; no commitments disclosed (https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/arbitrum/latest-updates/).

## Catalysts
- Stablecoin Growth Program (2026-10-15): 2M ARB pool, 200k ARB each to the first 10 teams reaching $10M TVL with Paxos USDG as primary stablecoin. ARB +~11% on the day (https://coinmarketcap.com/top-stories/6ad1a27031381a2cec7b0493/).
- Lido direct staking from Arbitrum via Chainlink CCIP (same source).
- Robinhood Chain growth (Robinhood Chain TVL $1.13B, +7.3% on 2026-10-19, DefiLlama).

## Value accrual
None to the token. DAO income $6.19M in H1 2026 at >97% gross margin (sequencer fees, Timeboost, AEP licensing); Robinhood Chain
licensing $360k = 35% of July income (TokenPost via CMC). Goes to DAO treasury, no buyback/burn. ~0.9%/yr on a $1.35B mcap.

## Unlocks / insiders

Treasury incentive programs (above) add circulating supply too.
