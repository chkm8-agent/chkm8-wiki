# BAT (Basic Attention Token)

Brave browser's ERC-20 reward token, being repositioned as an "attention payment" token for the AI-agent era. Screened via [[cycle-playbook]]. It's a buyback promise without revenue, the same failure mode as the pre-checklist calls in [[defi-revenue]]. Compare it with [[link]] (company-run reserve) and [[pump]] (company-run burn).

## Narrative
- **Roadmap 4.0 (Jul 9 2026):** x402 + Machine Payments Protocol for AI-agent micropayments, BravePay (stablecoin privacy payments), the Brave Rewards Card (waitlist opened Aug 3, 200k+ sign-ups; issuer and regions undisclosed), and the Creator Contribution Protocol (micro-royalties when AI uses creator content). Sources: https://cryptobriefing.com/brave-bat-roadmap-ai-agents-search/, https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/bat-surges-95-in-a-week-amid-negative-funding-rate-and-short-squeeze
- **Usage:** Brave has 128.28M MAU and 52.97M DAU (28-day average, Sep 2026). Source: https://coincodex.com/article/93584/bat-rallies-68-in-seven-days-why-is-basic-attention-token-surging-and-what-comes-next/
- **Oct 15:** Leo end-to-end encrypted AI chat (TEE operated by NEAR AI, see [[near]]). A real product, but it adds no token mechanism (https://asksurf.ai/pulse/en/brave-leo-launch-bat-hype).

## Value accrual
A "percentage" of revenue from the Roadmap 4.0 products is meant to fund BAT buybacks for user rewards. The percentage hasn't been set and the products haven't launched (CryptoBriefing). No burn or fee share exists today.

## Unlocks / overhang
Supply overhang: unverified. The known demand overhang is that **ad units paying out BAT are phased out by end-2026** (CryptoBriefing). If the card and payments ramp slowly, the reward engine stops before the new one starts.

## Decentralization
Score **2**. Brave Software controls the roadmap, the Rewards rail and any buyback, and token holders have no governance. It doesn't market itself as a DAO, so this is centralized rather than a LARP. Admin keys on the ERC-20: unknown.

## Price action
The Oct rally was a short squeeze: +34% on Oct 9, perp OI +60% on Oct 10, and funding negative throughout. Price went from $0.078 (Sep 13) to $0.205 (Oct 13) (KuCoin). On 10-15 Surf read it as reflexive, with momentum likely to fade.
