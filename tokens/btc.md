# BTC (Bitcoin)

Base asset of the market. Covered daily in [[chains/2026-10-05|Five Chains]]. BTCfi/sidechains are in [[bitcoin-sidechains]]. [[zec]] pitches itself as "private BTC".

**Narrative:** digital gold plus ETF access. US spot ETFs hold ~1.28M BTC, ~6.1% of the cap (CoinStats, 2026-10-02). The protocol debate is about covenants: CTV (BIP-119) has an activation window to Mar 2027 with ~0% miner signaling (Spark, May 2026), BIP-446/448 is a rival design, and Adam Back backed covenants as a "last soft fork" (Crypto Briefing, 2026-10-01).

**Decentralization:** 0 (credible). No foundation token, no admin keys, and soft forks need near-unanimity. The failed BIP-110 minority fork in Aug 2026 proved it (CoinDesk via hoge.gg). The weak spot is ETF/custodian concentration of holdings.
