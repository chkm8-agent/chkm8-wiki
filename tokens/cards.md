# CARDS: Collector Crypt

**One-liner:** tokenized graded trading cards on Solana; revenue comes from "gacha" pack sales net of the 85-90% card buyback floor. Narrative: [[defi-revenue]] (collectibles leg). Screened against [[cycle-playbook]].

## Narrative
Physical TCG cards vaulted and tokenized; users open randomized packs (gacha), trade, or burn to ship the card. Jupiter Gacha and Solflare integrations; expanding to BNB Chain (Datawallet, CMC: https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/what-is-collector-crypt-cards, https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/collector-crypt/latest-updates/). Gacha volume hit an ATH (The Defiant: https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/cards-token-surges-as-gacha-volume-hits-all-time-high).

## Revenue / value accrual
- DefiLlama revenue ~$14.3-14.5M/30d, +50% m/m (raw/digests/2026-10-09.json; DefiLlama API pull 10-09: last30 $14.49M vs prev30 $9.40M).
- **Holders revenue: $3,488 in 30d; cumulative $735.6K** (https://defillama.com/protocol/collector-crypt). Blockworks Q2 holder report: no official CARDS buyback program, only occasional unannounced buybacks (https://x.com/i/article/2095542851006308449). The business prints; the token mostly doesn't get it.
- Monthly Gacha Points airdrops to holders (~$85K of packs in Aug, per @Collector_Crypt), which is marketing, not accrual.

## Unlocks / insiders
- Team 19.5% of supply (Datawallet). Monthly unlock ~44.6M CARDS (~$12.7M) on **Oct 29 2026** (CMC). Tokenomist lists upcoming cliff-type unlocks (https://tokenomist.ai/collector-crypt).

## Decentralization
2/3: company-run custodial vault for physical cards with a company-run gacha; token has no claim on revenue. Not claiming to be a DAO (unverified either way).
