# CRV (Curve DAO)

Curve: stableswap/cryptoswap DEX + crvUSD stablecoin + LlamaLend lending. Narratives: [[defi-revenue]]; screened via [[cycle-playbook]].

## Narrative
"DeFi's original dividend token, with shrinking emissions." Q3 2026: active loans +68% to $70.9M (LlamaLend v2 on Ethereum/Optimism, ~84% of net debt growth) while DeFi lending shrank; yRisk got a 12-month crvUSD/LlamaLend risk mandate. (https://coinmarketcap.com/top-stories/6ad107a231381a2cec7a605d)

## Catalysts
- Grayscale DeFi Fund via Republic (Oct 15 2026) holds CRV at 4.42% weight; accredited investors only, not an ETF. (CMC, above)
- Annual emission cut each August; Aug 2026 to ~97.2M CRV/yr (~4% of supply), first time <100M. (https://tapbit.com/en/learn/article/curve-crv-revenue-emissions-crvusd-llamalend-20260929)

## Value accrual
veCRV gets 50% of DEX admin fees + 100% of crvUSD revenue (https://tokenomics.com/articles/curve-tokenomics-the-vecrv-model-that-defined-defi-value-accrual).
DefiLlama curve-dex holders revenue, rolling 30d windows (newest first, as of 2026-10-20): $1.67M, $0.88M, $1.17M, $0.96M, $1.50M, $1.47M.
Paid in weekly lumps (daily chart spikes $140-340k), so m/m % is noisy. ~$20M/yr to holders vs ~$36M/yr emissions at $0.37: still net inflationary.

## Unlocks / insiders
No cliff unlocks; supply grows only via the hardcoded emission schedule (max 3.03B). Top 100 wallets ~82% of supply (CMC AI, single source: https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/curve-dao-token/price-prediction/).

## Decentralization
Score 1: on-chain DAO with real fee routing, but voting dominated by Convex/Yearn/StakeDAO (rejected a $6.6M dev grant in 2026, per CMC AI, unverified). Founder concentration was historically an issue.
