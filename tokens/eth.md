# ETH (Ethereum)

Settlement layer for most DeFi (see [[defi-revenue]], [[uni]], [[sky]], [[fluid]]). Covered daily in [[chains/2026-10-05|Five Chains]].

**Narrative:** L1 scaling is back. Glamsterdam (ePBS EIP-7732 + block access lists EIP-7928 + state-gas repricing) hits Sepolia on 2026-10-06, Hoodi is tentatively Oct 27 and mainnet is "Q4, unconfirmed". EF calls a 200M gas floor credible (crypto.news, CCN). Next fork after that: Hegotá (2027).

**Decentralization:** 1. Client diversity and ePBS reduce trusted relay middleware. Concentration lives in staking (Lido/CEXs) and in the EF's soft power over the roadmap.
