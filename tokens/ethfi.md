# ETHFI (Ether.fi)

Largest liquid-restaking protocol by TVL (~$3.4B), expanded into a crypto debit/credit card business ("Cash") that now
drives most of its revenue. Screened against [[cycle-playbook]]. Restaking sector led today's sector table (+2.9%).

## Narrative
"Liquid restaking that pivoted into a crypto debit card." The Cash card business is ~65% of protocol revenue
(Cash spend ~$100M/month per PANews July figures) and funds a programmatic ETHFI buyback.

## Value accrual
A live buyback exists: eETH withdrawal fees fund **weekly** ETHFI purchases, plus **monthly** purchases from a share
of broader protocol revenue (ether.fi docs). But per TechFlow (Oct 18), the buyback has missed its own guidance by
~27% (actual ~$975k/mo vs $1.33M/mo target) while Cash spend kept growing, and nearly all purchased tokens get
redistributed as cashback rather than held — recipients cashed out ~70% of distributed tokens, and the week of
Oct 12 was the first week outflows (1.28M ETHFI) exceeded purchases (ETHFI retained in Foundation: 548k).
This is closer to a **customer-acquisition cost paid in ETHFI** than a PUMP/UNI-style supply-reducing burn.

## Revenue
DefiLlama (via Sector Alpha, data to 2026-10-23): $12.2M fees / **$3.8M revenue** over 30 days; $37.8M fees / $10.6M
revenue over 90 days. Real, non-incentive revenue — passes the mandatory revenue screen, unlike QNT/GRASS/VVV.
