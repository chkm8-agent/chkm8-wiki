# FLUID (Fluid, formerly Instadapp)

A lending + DEX "liquidity layer" on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon and (via Jupiter Lend) Solana. Narrative: [[defi-revenue]] (revenue → buyback),
screened against [[cycle-playbook]]. It's a peer of [[uni]] and [[aero]] in the DEX-revenue cohort.

## Narrative
"The DeFi liquidity layer, priced like it died." Fluid claims it's the #2 DEX on Ethereum by volume and fees, and the top-3 lending market with $2.5B of active loans.
Its target was $30M annualized revenue (from $15M+) (fluid.io/blog/general-fluid-reserve, Jul 2026; self-reported).

## Catalysts
- Jupiter Lend "Smart Debt" built on Fluid infrastructure (Aug 12 2026) (CMC AI news, coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/instadapp/latest-updates/).
- Kinetic Group (UAE asset manager) plans open-market purchases of up to 10% of supply (~$11.7M), announced Jul 24 2026 (same CMC page). Execution unverified.
- DEX v2 and Solana expansion (Ainvest, Jul 17 2026).

## Value accrual
The Fluid Reserve buyback launched Oct 2025 with 100% of Ethereum mainnet revenue (gate.com/news/detail/14510425). Bought tokens go to a **reserve, not a burn**.
It was **paused** after $21M of bad debt (May 2026) and a $215K key-compromise exploit (Jun 2026), pending a treasury rebuild. Restart is tied to ~$10M annualized revenue
(ainvest.com/news/fluid-fluid-3-5-24h-instadapp-defi-hybrid-gathers-steam-buyback-thesis-survive-rebuild-2607/). Current buyback status: unknown.

## Unlocks / insiders
Fully vested (schedule ended 2025). 83.7M of 100M circulating. Allocation: community 55%, current team 23.79%, investors 12.09%, future team/ecosystem 7.85%, advisors 1.27%
(tokenomist.ai/instadapp). There's no unlock overhang, but ~16M non-circulating tokens are controlled by the team/treasury.

## Decentralization
**1-2/3 (unverified).** It calls itself a DAO, but a "Fluid Foundation" signs strategic deals (Kinetic), and a June key compromise implies privileged keys exist. Needs checking.
