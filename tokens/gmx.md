# GMX

The original oracle-priced, pool-based perps DEX (Arbitrum and Avalanche), now also on Solana as **GMTrade** (it lists crypto plus RWA, FX and commodity perps). Peer set: [[hype]], [[lit]]. Revenue context: [[defi-revenue]]. Screened against [[cycle-playbook]].

## Narrative
"The OG perps DEX found a second life on Solana." GMTrade does ~$23.2B in 30d perp volume (DefiLlama). It ranked #4 perp DEX by 24h volume on 2026-10-02 (cipher-intelligence.io), out-trading GMX on Arbitrum.

## Catalysts
- GMTrade volume growth on Solana.
- A governance plan to "restore price discovery" with a 1M GMX treasury buy-wall at $5 (GMX on X: https://x.com/GMX_IO/status/2029238665382076418).

## Value accrual
- **Buy Back & Distribute (BB&D):** >2M GMX repurchased since late 2024 (GMX on X). DefiLlama shows token buybacks of $724.8K in Q3 2026 and $2.66M in Q4-to-date (https://defillama.com/protocol/gmx).
- **Caveat (2026-10-18):** DefiLlama's "GMX Solana" revenue spike from Sep 28 (from ~$15-35k/day to $120-520k/day; 30d $3.88M, "+327% m/m") is mostly **GT Buyback Season 2**. That's GMTrade's *own* token GT sold to the treasury, counted as holders revenue. **GT is not GMX** (DefiLlama methodology, gmx-sol.ts adapter). So it isn't clear how much Solana activity reaches GMX holders.

## Unlocks / insiders
10.68M circulating of 13.25M max (CoinGecko, 2026-10-18), so ~81% is circulating. We found no large dated unlock. Still, the 2.57M gap to max supply is unexplained here (unverified).

## Decentralization
Score **unknown**. GMX DAO governs, but we haven't checked admin/upgrade keys. GMTrade appears to have a separate token and treasury (GT). History: a $565k V1 price manipulation (Sep 2022) and a **$42M V1 reentrancy hack (Jul 2025)** (DefiLlama hacks).
