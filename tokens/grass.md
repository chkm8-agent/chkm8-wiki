# GRASS (Grass)

Solana-based DePIN: users share idle home bandwidth, and Grass uses it to scrape public web data. It sells that data to AI labs as training sets and now as live retrieval for agents (Live Context Retrieval, LCR). Screened via [[cycle-playbook]]. It has the same failure mode as [[vvv]]: a real business, but no token claim on its revenue. Compare with [[bat]] (an AI-payments roadmap with a buyback that's only promised).

## Narrative
"Live web data for AI agents." The Contents API launched Oct 8, 2026 and turns any public URL into model-ready text. In Grass's own 100-URL benchmark it retrieved 97%, against 84% for Firecrawl, 76% for Parallel and 66% for Exa ([Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/grass-launches-contents-api-a-live-web-retrieval-tool-for-ai-agents), [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/30436)). Multicoin invested in late September. The CEO says LCR serves 4M+ external requests per day, and a Search API is in the works ([FalconX](https://www.falconx.io/newsroom/grass-unlocking-live-data-access-for-the-ai-agent-boom)).

## Revenue (offchain, self-reported)
- H2 2025: $14.3M. H1 2026: $14.5M verified, or $17-18M by Grass's own count. That's only about +19% half over half ([Milk Road](https://milkroad.com/news/grass-revenue-is-real-but-token-sink-is-broken/), [FalconX](https://www.falconx.io/newsroom/grass-unlocking-live-data-access-for-the-ai-agent-boom)).
- FY26 guidance is $65-75M, which needs about $52M in H2. Not yet disclosed (FalconX).
- It's not on DefiLlama, so we can't track it month on month. Item 1 is only partly verifiable.

## Value accrual
- **None formalized.** Revenue goes to Grass DataCo, a subsidiary of the memberless Grass Foundation, and most of it is reinvested. The only buyback was a one-time $350K in Nov/Dec 2025, about 1% of revenue ([Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/newsletter/0xresearch/issue/post_d97aaf3a-37bb-46d6-9f0a-8d3ac5695b0f), [grass.io July 7 call](https://www.grass.io/learn/july-7-grass-token-holder-and-network-participant-call/)).
- Stage 2 node rewards are paid in USDC out of revenue, so they add no new GRASS supply. That's neutral to positive.
- RootData mentions a "proposed governance vote on revenue sharing." Unverified, with no date ([RootData](https://www.rootdata.com/projects/detail/Grass?k=ODUzNQ==)).

## Unlocks / overhang
- Circulating supply is 699.5M of 1B (CoinGecko, 10-25).
- **Oct 28 unlock:** about 20M GRASS (~2.8% of circulating), the last monthly early-investor release. After it, about 10.5M GRASS a month of investor supply stops (FalconX, citing DefiLlama).

## Decentralization
**2/3.** All the IP, contracts and revenue sit with a foundation that has no members. Wynd Labs (45 FTE) is a paid vendor and takes about 20% of H1 spend. There's no equity, which is better than most company-run tokens. But revenue is offchain and self-reported, the token has no enforceable claim, and the Foundation decides on any buybacks. Sources are above.
