# INJ: Injective

**One-liner:** "The SEC-registered L1 for tokenized stocks." A Cosmos-SDK finance chain (WASM + EVM) pivoting hard into regulated RWA issuance, with US ETF filings pending. Screened against [[cycle-playbook]]; adjacent to the tokenized-stock / Robinhood Chain theme in [[defi-revenue]].

## Narrative / catalysts
- **SEC transfer agent:** Injective Institutional Services' registration is now **effective**. The filing had been announced earlier, and the effective notice came out around Oct 14-15 2026. It lets the affiliate keep official securities ownership records onchain. Sources: https://injective.com/blog/sec-transfer-agent, https://cryptobriefing.com/injective-sec-registered-transfer-agent-layer-1/
- **ETFs:** 21Shares Injective ETF (Nasdaq: TINJ), S-1/A filed Sep 18 2026 (the original was Oct 2025). Canary Staked INJ ETF (Cboe: INJS), Amendment 3 around Sep 25-28, staking at least 90%, BitGo custody. https://cryptobriefing.com/injective-ceo-us-inj-etfs-before-2027/. The EU already has the 21Shares AINJ ETP, but its AUM is only about $2.3M (21shares.com), so existing wrapper demand is small.
- **Product:** the Meridian upgrade (IIP-701, Sep 24) adds a regulated token standard and private RFQ testing. Onchain tokenized stocks launched Sep 23, and Injective Mint (private alpha since Jul 22) handles RWA issuance. MiCA white paper listed on ESMA's register. (https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/injective/latest-updates/)

## Value accrual
A monthly **Community BuyBack**: participants commit INJ, it is burned, and they get rewards (the Sep 30 round paid out tokenized Robinhood Chain stocks). How big each burn is matters, and it is unverified here.

## Revenue
DefiLlama "Injective" chain revenue is about **$235k per 30 days** (fees about the same), with no m/m change field (api.llama.fi, 2026-10-16). Helix revenue is negligible on DefiLlama. That's tiny next to a $911M cap. It **fails item 1** of the playbook (mandatory since 10-07).

## Unlocks / insiders
Circulating supply equals total supply at 100M (CoinGecko 2026-10-16), so there's **no vesting overhang**. Staking yield was 9.3-13.6% in 2025 (Binance News), so issuance is the remaining supply pressure, partly offset by burns.

## Decentralization
2/3. The PoS validator set is public, but the regulated rails (transfer agent, Mint, protocol-level transfer controls) are run by Injective company affiliates. Permissioned assets on a "permissionless" chain: fine for RWAs, just don't call it neutral.
