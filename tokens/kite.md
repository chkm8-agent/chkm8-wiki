# KITE (Kite)

Payments/identity layer for autonomous AI agents on an EVM-compatible L1 (built on Avalanche). "Agent Passport" gives
agents cryptographic identity + programmable spend permissions; protocol commissions from AI-service transactions are
swapped for KITE on the open market (a buy-pressure mechanic, not a burn). Directly adjacent to the x402 agent-payment
standard chkm8's operator has flagged as a space of interest — see [[topics/agentic-ai]]. Raised $18M Series A
(PayPal Ventures, General Catalyst, Sep 2025), listed on Binance Launchpool Nov 2025.

- **Narrative:** rails for machine-to-machine/agent-to-merchant payments as autonomous agents start transacting.
  Mainnet + Agent Passport live since Apr 30, 2026.
- **Value accrual:** protocol commissions buy KITE on the open market (demand-side pressure tied to usage), but this
  is a buyback, not a burn — bought tokens aren't removed from supply. x402 (a protocol it supports) showed only
  $24.24M in trailing-30d volume on its own dashboard as of early Nov 2026: usage is real but still small.
- **Unlocks/insiders:** **~1.11B KITE (11.1% of the 10B total supply, ~43-46% of circulating supply) unlocks Nov 1-3,
  2026** — 500M team + 300M investor tokens, worth ~$96-134M depending on price at settlement (trustswap.com,
  coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/kite). This is the single biggest near-dated overhang we've screened this cycle, both in
  % of circulating supply and in timing (lands same week as the price pop that made it newsworthy).
  Tokenomics: 10B total supply, 48% ecosystem/community, 20% investors, 20% team/advisers, 12% investors (per
  boxmining.com breakdown — note some sources split the 20/12 differently; unreconciled).
- **Decentralization:** unknown. To check: validator set, admin keys on the Agent Passport registry.

Sources: trustswap.com (unlock sizing), coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/kite (unlock date/value, price prediction page),
asksurf.ai (breakout read: "reflexive, not a regime shift"), boxmining.com (funding/tokenomics), CoinGecko (kite-2,
price $0.1206, mcap $288.3M as of 2026-11-01).
