# KNTQ (Kinetiq)

**One-liner:** Hyperliquid's own liquid-staking protocol token — stake HYPE for kHYPE, keep it liquid across DeFi.
KNTQ is the governance token and "sole instrument" of value accrual. Ecosystem peer: [[hype]]. Screened against
[[cycle-playbook]].

## Narrative
Kinetiq launched on Hyperliquid Nov 27, 2025. Core product is liquid staking (HYPE → kHYPE); it also runs Markets
(onchain perps) and Launch (crowdsourced HIP-3 LST deployments). First major token launch in the Kinetiq/
Hyperliquid LST ecosystem (kinetiq.xyz).

## Revenue / value accrual
- Revenue sources: validator commissions (validators remit 50% of commissions earned from protocol delegation),
  staking performance fee (10%), and Markets/Launch trading fees.
- 70% of protocol revenue funds KNTQ buybacks (KIP-2); 30% to treasury. 100% of validator-commission buybacks
  and 100% of KNTQ trading fees also route to buybacks/burn.
- **KIP-5 (recent):** previously, bought-back KNTQ was distributed to sKNTQ stakers (generated ~13% APY). KIP-5
  redirects all future buybacks to a permanent system burn address (`0xfefe...fefe`, the same Hyperliquid
  Assistance Fund address pattern), permanently removing them from supply instead of recycling to stakers. ~5.39M
  KNTQ (~0.54% of supply) already bought at avg $0.15 before the change (kinetiq.xyz/blog/kip-5-kntq-buybacks-assistance-fund).
  This is the strongest buyback+burn mechanic we've screened on this chain.

## Unlocks / insiders
- Fixed max supply 1B. Allocation: 30% protocol growth/rewards, 25% initial airdrop (points-weighted), 23.5% core
  contributors, 10% foundation, 7.5% investors, 4% liquidity. Insider+foundation ≈ 41%.
- Insider/investor vesting: 1-year cliff + 2-year monthly linear release. **Cliff ends ~Nov 27, 2026** (~29 days
  out from this digest): ~4.09% of circulating supply unlocking at once (~$5-6M at current mcap, per
  tokentoria.com/cryptolaxy.com unlock trackers).
- ~32% of max supply circulating as of this writing (CoinGecko).
