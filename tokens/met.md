# MET (Meteora)

Solana liquidity infrastructure (DLMM, DAMM v2, Dynamic Bonding Curve launch pools). Rebrand of Mercurial (FTX-era SAFT).
Narrative: [[defi-revenue]] (Solana DEX revenue leg, alongside [[ray]]). Screened against [[cycle-playbook]].

## Narrative
"Solana's liquidity plumbing": the LP Army + launchpad pools that memecoin and new-asset launches route through.
About 80% of DEX volume comes from memecoins/new launches/emerging assets (Blockworks transparency filing), so revenue is Solana-speculation beta.

## Catalysts
- DLMM protocol fee share doubled 5% → 10% on 2026-05-19; depositor retention unchanged (48.5% vs 48.4%) and revenue per unit of volume ~doubled (H1 2026 Token Holder Report, https://ir.meteora.ag/assets/Meteora_H1_2026_Token_Holder_Report.pdf).
- LIBRA class action dismissed 2026-10-02 (CMC AI news, https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/meteora/latest-updates/).
- Meteora DLMM revenue $2.69M/30d, +85.2% m/m (DefiLlama via raw/digests/2026-10-08.json).

## Value accrual
- Discretionary buybacks from revenue: 36.0M MET bought since Oct 2025; holdings 38.8M (3.9% of supply) on 06-30; **no buybacks in Q2 2026** (H1 report).
- Referral staking (live 07-21): 10% of DLMM protocol fee to staking rewards + 10% to referral rewards; 70.0M MET staked early Aug (H1 report).
- Mint authority burned, fixed 1B supply (Blockworks filing).

## Unlocks / insiders
- Team 18% + Meteora Reserve 34%, 1-month cliff then 72-month linear vest, to 2031-10-23 (https://docs.meteora.ag/protocol/met/tokenomics).
- Runs at 7,222,222 MET/month (0.72% of supply): 2.5M team + 4.72M reserve. Team tokens moved to Jupiter Lock, vesting to individuals through 2027-02-28 (H1 report).
- 48% circulating at TGE (Oct 23 2025).

## Decentralization
Score **2/3**. The 34% reserve sits in a team 4/7 multisig; the team sets fee parameters and runs buybacks/treasury; "There is no DAO" and no onchain governance (Blockworks transparency filing, https://blockworks.com/token-transparency/filing/meteora). Point in their favor: they say so plainly instead of LARPing as a DAO.
