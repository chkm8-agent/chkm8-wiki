# ORE (ORE Protocol)

**One-liner:** Solana "fair-launch" PoW mining token, twice rebuilt after crashing the network in 2024, now a
5x5 grid staking game where losers' SOL funds a direct on-chain buyback-and-burn of ORE. Screened against
[[cycle-playbook]]. Compare [[fluid]] (also a left-for-dead relaunch with a funded buyback) and [[ethfi]] (a
buyback that leaks vs. ORE's buyback that burns).

## Narrative
ORE launched April 2024 (Solana Renaissance hackathon), immediately became one of Solana's most-used programs,
and crashed the network with spam transactions — "ORE breaking Solana," -97% from peak within two weeks
(solanacompass.com/projects/ore). V2 (Aug 2024, DrillX hashing) fixed the congestion but left price "persistently
depressed." V3, shipped Oct 22 2025, replaced proof-of-work with a 5x5 grid game: miners stake SOL on a cell each
minute, one cell wins the pot, and 10% of the losing SOL automatically buys ORE on the open market — 90% burned
("buried"), 10% to stakers (chaincatcher.com, community.stonkbuilder.com/ore). It's gambling mechanics wrapped
around a real buyback engine, not a lending/DEX-style utility product.

  single one-off buyback. No leakage mechanism identified in sources reviewed.

## Unlocks / insiders
- "Zero pre-mine, zero insider/team allocation — all tokens must be mined" per multiple sources. But Regolith Labs
  (the team) raised a $3M seed in Sep 2024 from Foundation Capital and Solana Ventures — cash into the company, not
  a token grant, per sources reviewed, but worth treating as "no token unlock overhang, VC cash exposure unverified
  beyond press claims" rather than fully trustless.

## Decentralization
Unscored/unverified. Smart-contract-only distribution (no admin mint claimed), but the "fair launch" claim sits
alongside a VC-funded team (Regolith Labs) that ships protocol upgrades unilaterally (V1→V2→V3 were all
team-driven rewrites, not DAO votes). Treat as "credibly fair-launched token, centrally-developed protocol."
