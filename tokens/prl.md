# PRL: Pearl

**One-liner:** "Bitcoin, but the mining is AI matrix math." A proof-of-useful-work L1 where GPU miners secure the chain by running the matrix multiplications that AI training and inference use. Narrative: AI compute (see [[wal]] for the adjacent AI-data leg). Screened against [[cycle-playbook]].

## Narrative
- Pearl Research Labs launched mainnet on Apr 27 2026. The node is a fork of btcd (UTXO model, Taproot-only `prl1` addresses, OP_CAT enabled, a dormant XMSS post-quantum backup key). Miners run "NoisyGEMM/cuPOW" matrix multiplications on Nvidia GPUs and prove the work with a ZK certificate. The scheme comes from a 2025 paper by co-founders Komargodski and Weinstein (arxiv 2504.09971). Sources: https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/pearl-explained, https://oysterwallet.app/blog/what-is-pearl-prl
- **Catalyst:** in May, Together AI launched a Pearl-powered model priced 25% cheaper, with PRL mining rewards covering the discount. The ATH was about $1.65 on May 29 after that announcement, and the price then fell as difficulty rose (https://iq.wiki/wiki/pearl-prl-layer-1-blockchain-with-proof-of-useful-work). Blockonomi reports a later high of about $2.10 after a 228% monthly gain (https://blockonomi.com/pearl-prl-price-token-touches-2-10-after-228-monthly-gain).
- It trades only on SafeTrade, BigONE and CoinEx, with no Binance or Coinbase listing (oysterwallet). Beware the unrelated BSC "Pearl" token for CaptainCrypto.

## Value accrual
None beyond being the chain's money. Fees go to miners, and there's no burn or buyback. The open economic question is whether the discount is paid by inference demand or simply subsidized by emissions.

## Unlocks / supply
- Hard cap 2.1B. **No premine and no team or VC allocation**: all PRL comes from mining (datawallet, blockonomi).
- About 331.9M was mined by Oct 2 (15.8% of the cap). Emissions follow a smooth curve, with about 50% of supply in the first 4 years. Issuance is about 1.0-1.2M PRL a day, and **about 305M more arrives in the next 12 months, nearly double the current supply** (blockonomi). This is a known, dated overhang, and a big one: miners are natural sellers, the same way they are on BTC.

## Decentralization
1/3 (unverified). There's no insider allocation, and issuance is set in node code. Pearl Research Labs publishes the node, the wallet and the explorer, and the miner set is unknown.
