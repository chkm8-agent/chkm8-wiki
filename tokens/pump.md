# PUMP (Pump.fun)

Token of Pump.fun, the Solana memecoin launchpad. Narrative: [[defi-revenue]] (revenue → buy-and-burn). Screened against [[cycle-playbook]].
It's a competitor of the launchpads that feed [[ray]] (StonkFun) and the source of Raydium's lost graduation flow (PumpSwap).

- **Narrative:** "The casino burns half its take." It's a memecoin factory with a buyback mechanic that's real and transparent.
- **Usage:** DefiLlama pump.fun revenue $34.79M/30d (-4.9% m/m) on 2026-10-04, plus pump.fun Mobile App $3.79M (+29.9%). Pump group about $52.9M/30d and $16.1M/7d to Sep 29 (cryptoticker.io/en/pump-fun-buyback-sec-faq).
- **Value accrual:** 50% of revenue goes to open-market PUMP buys, which are **burned**. Cumulative spend is ~$466-473M, and 169.7B PUMP (~17% of the 1T max) has been burned as of Oct 3. Daily buys were $1.0-1.2M from Sep 28 to Oct 3. The 50% share is programmatic for one year from 2026-04-28; after that it can change. Sources: https://happycoin.news/en/pump-token-whales-buyback-burn-pumpfunu-buyback-pump-fun-vazhnee-dvuh-krupnyh-koshelkov, https://startupfortune.com/pumpfun-has-burned-17-of-its-token-supply-and-the-sec-just-cleared-the-path
- **Catalysts:** SEC Corp Fin staff FAQ (2026-09-25): a buyback alone isn't "managerial effort" if the network is functional. A Sep 28 follow-up adds a no-central-party condition, which Pump.fun arguably fails (startupfortune, cryptoticker). Custom Pairs launched Sep 10: 93 quote assets, including tokenized stocks, with 50% of revenue to buybacks.
- **Unlocks/insiders:** Team 20% (200B) and existing investors 13% (130B) vest on a cliff + linear schedule. The first major insider unlock was 57.3B PUMP (~$86M) on 2026-07-15 (tokenomist.ai/pump-fun, Yahoo/99Bitcoins). Circulating is ~465B, and ~365B (44%) is still with team, investors and reserves (cryptoticker).
- **Decentralization: 3/3 on control, 0 on pretense.** A company sets fees and buyback size. It doesn't really claim to be decentralized, so it isn't LARPing, but holders have no say.
- **ATH distance:** unknown (not in today's data).
