# PYTH (Pyth Network)

Oracle network (price feeds for DeFi) pivoting into a market-data subscription business. Spot
candidate flagged 2026-11-06 (see [[cycle-playbook]]). Compare to other revenue-buyback stories:
[[hype]], [[lit]], [[zro]].

- **Narrative:** Pyth Pro (tiered subscriptions, $500-$2,500+/mo), Listing-as-a-Service, Data
  Marketplace and Pyth Indices — "turning an oracle into a Bloomberg-style data business."
- **Catalysts:** OP-PIP-136 (DAO vote passed Oct 8 2026, first purchases executed Sep 30) commits
  **100%** of the DAO's product-revenue share to open-market PYTH buybacks, replacing a prior
  monthly one-third-of-treasury draw (cryptopolitan.com, rootdata.com).
- **Value accrual:** real revenue (group ARR $13.67M Oct 2026, up from $11.49M Sep, +86% q/q —
  solanacompass.com, forum.pyth.network) funds the buyback, but purchased PYTH goes into a
  **non-sellable DAO reserve (~42-56M tokens)**, not a burn — supply isn't actually reduced. October's
  DAO revenue share was only ~$621K (forum.pyth.network), small next to a ~$520-590M mcap.
  Decentralization note: revenue depends on a concentrated set of institutional data publishers —
  a flagged single point of failure (rootdata.com).
- **Unlocks/insiders:** 78.75% of the 10B max supply already circulating (tokenomist.ai, levex.com);
  insiders in the narrow sense (private sales + protocol development) are ~20% of total supply.
  Final unlock cliff is **May 19, 2027** (~2.125B tokens, the last of four 6/18/30/42-month cliffs) —
  distant and already well-telegraphed.
- **Decentralization:** score unknown/not formally assessed; publisher concentration is the main
  flagged risk, not team/insider control.
