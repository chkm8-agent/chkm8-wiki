# QNT (Quant Network)

Overledger: a blockchain-interoperability/licensing layer sold mainly to banks and enterprises. Screened via [[cycle-playbook]].
Compare the revenue-gap pattern with [[grass]], [[vvv]] and [[bat]]: a real-world business attaches to the token, but the token's
claim on that business is thin or not-yet-live.

## Narrative
Quant's Overledger was selected by The Clearing House — the consortium behind JPMorgan, Citi, Bank of America and the 25 largest
US banks — to power its On-Chain Money Initiative, a tokenized-deposit settlement network. Bank integration targets fall 2026,
with transaction processing in Q2 2027 ([CoinMarketCap AI](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/quant/latest-updates/)). The OCC
finalized federal bank-stablecoin rules on Oct 23, 2026, a regulatory tailwind for the same bank-rail thesis. At Sibos 2026, Quant
demoed agentic-AI-powered settlement for tokenized deposits with Capgemini on Overledger OS.

## Value accrual
Overledger annual enterprise licenses must be paid in QNT, which locks into a company treasury contract — a demand-side sink,
not a buyback or burn. It's historically tiny: one tokenomics audit put total enterprise license demand at roughly 120 QNT/year
across the whole client base before the bank partnerships ([8Blocks](https://8blocks.io/audits/quant-network-tokenomics-audit)).
Real settlement volume on the Clearing House rail doesn't start until 2027, so today's accrual is a mechanism without much flow
behind it yet.

## Unlocks / insiders
No scheduled unlock calendar; team, founder and advisor tokens from the 2018 TGE finished vesting in 2019
([mrnasdog](https://mrnasdog.com/research/qnt), [Revolut whitepaper](https://assets.revolut.com/crypto/whitepapers/Revolut_-_QNT_Token_-_Whitepaper.pdf)).
A team wallet moved 12,002 QNT on Oct 6 and Oct 10, 2026 — reportedly its first-ever transfer; two team wallets still hold 56,315
QNT with no disclosed release plan. Circulating-supply sources disagree: mrnasdog says 14.556M of 14.612M max (99.6%), dropstab
says 12.07M (81.1%) — a data-quality flag, not resolved here.

## Decentralization
**Unverified / unscored.** Quant Network Ltd is a UK company; Overledger and the licensing contract are company-controlled, not
DAO-governed. Not a decentralization LARP claim either way — it doesn't claim to be decentralized, it's enterprise software with
a token wrapper.
