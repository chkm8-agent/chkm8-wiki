# RAY (Raydium)

Solana's largest legacy DEX (AMM v4, CPMM, CLMM, LaunchLab launchpad, perps). Narrative: [[defi-revenue]].
Screened against [[cycle-playbook]] on 2026-10-03 (5/6). Peer: [[uni]] (same "fees → token" re-rating).

## Narrative
"Lost its biggest customer, found a new one." After Pump.fun launched PumpSwap (Mar 2025) and stopped migrating
graduations, quarterly fees fell from ~$412M (Q4'24) to ~$17M (Q2'26). StonkFun (integrated Sep 5 2026) and tokenized
US-equity listings pushed 30d fees back to ~$41.7M and RAY ~+150% in 30 days.
Sources: https://fintrender.com/en/reports/a-case-for-ray, https://themerkle.com/raydiums-holders-revenue-reaches-443m-as-stonkfun-drives-launchlab-volume

## Catalysts
- LaunchLab volume from StonkFun; tokenized-stock pools. Not owned distribution (StonkFun can leave like Pump.fun did).
- Possible: burning the accumulated buyback stack (Fintrender's bull case). Not proposed as far as we know (unverified).

## Value accrual
12% of AMM/CLMM/CPMM trading fees buy back RAY (https://docs.raydium.io/ray/ray-buybacks); LaunchLab 25% of protocol
fee buy-and-burn (DefiLlama methodology). Holder revenue $4.43M/30d; $640K single-day buyback in Sep (The Merkle).
Bought-back RAY **accumulates** at DdHDoz94o2WJmD9myRobHCwtx1bESpHTd4SSPe6VEZaz, >30% of circulating; not burned.

## Unlocks / insiders
Mint authority disabled; emissions ~1.9M RAY/yr; no VC round disclosed, no cliff left (Fintrender). Clean.

## Decentralization
Score **2**: programs upgradeable by a 3-of-4 multisig (24h timelock); 12% buyback share is policy set by a 3-of-5
treasury multisig with **no timelock**; holders have no enforceable claim on the buyback stack (Fintrender).
Immutable disabled mint is the one hard guarantee.
