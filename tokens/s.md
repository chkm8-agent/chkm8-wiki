# S (Sonic)

Sonic (ex-Fantom) L1, run by Sonic Labs. Recurring green name during the [[l2-shutdowns]] squeeze/unwind (10-11 +17.0%, 10-12 +21.1%, 10-13 +14.3%).
Screened against [[cycle-playbook]]: fails item 1 (no revenue routed to holders). Compare [[zro]] (buybacks live) and [[pump]] (revenue burn).

## Narrative
"The team stopped printing." CEO Matt Visser ended all manual S issuance; new supply now only from automated validator emissions,
and commissioned an unnamed third-party audit of total supply + reporting (https://www.3versetv.com/news/sonic-orders-independent-audit-after-cancelling-manual-s-token-mints/).
Findings due by 2026-10-13 (https://www.tradingview.com/news/coinmarketcal:63c8ecccb094b:0-sonic-supply-audit-report-concludes-with-published-findings-by-13-oct-2026/).

## Catalysts
- Supply audit publication (~Oct 13): binary, can move either way.
- 32.69M unclaimed airdrop S burnable by anyone after 2026-10-15, ~0.9% of circulating, one-off (https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/04/02/sonic-tokens-burn/ , https://cryptoticker.io/en/sonic-airdrop-claim-deadline/).

## Unlocks / insiders
