# SKY (Sky Ecosystem)

Governance token of Sky (formerly MakerDAO): issuer of USDS and the yield-bearing sUSDS, with Spark/Grove as "Prime Agents".
Narratives: [[defi-revenue]], stablecoins/RWA. Screened via [[cycle-playbook]] on 2026-10-02 (4/6).

## Narrative
"The onchain savings bank TradFi balance sheets plug into." Galaxy Digital added **$100M sUSDS** to its corporate treasury,
approved it as collateral across a ~$1.4B loan book, and bought an undisclosed amount of SKY. Grove already gives Galaxy a
$500M warehouse facility. Sources: The Block 2026-09-23 (https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-09-23-galaxy-adds-100-million-in-skys-susds-to-treasury-buys-sky-token-as-firms-deepen-lending-ties-416137),
https://xbt.market/2026/09/25/galaxy-adds-100m-of-skys-susds-to-corporate-treasury

## Catalysts
- Institutional sUSDS adoption (Galaxy). S&P assigned a B- rating (per Crypto Economy).
- Tokenization Grand Prix: $1B RWA onboarding, with an OUSG (Ondo/Securitize/BlackRock) submission (CMC/CryptoBriefing).
- Public-company holder: SDEV (ex-NovaBay, NASDAQ) bought ~8.78% of circulating SKY in March 2026 (Sky insights).

## Value accrual
Smart Burn Engine buybacks: **$124.7M spent, 1.98B SKY, 7.76% of circulating**, about $73K/day recently (https://info.sky.money/buyback).
**Caveat:** on 2026-03-14 governance cut buybacks from 75% to 7.5% of net profit to build Aggregate Backstop Capital
(target $125M). Staking rewards are now paid from SKY reserves, which is effectively emissions. The long-term plan is 25% buyback / 25% USDS to
stakers / 50% retained. Source: https://insights.skyeco.com/insights/sky-ecosystem-monthly-financial-operational-update

## Unlocks / insiders
No VC cliff known (legacy MKR token converted). Concentration: SDEV ~8.78% of circulating; 74% of supply is staked (info.sky.money).

## Decentralization
Score **unknown (lean 1)**. Onchain SKY governance does execute parameter changes (the buyback cut). But large-holder concentration
and the RWA/credit stack (Grove, Spark) mean offchain counterparties and legal entities matter. To research: governance voter
concentration and any emergency/admin powers.
