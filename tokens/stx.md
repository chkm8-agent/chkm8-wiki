# STX (Stacks)

Bitcoin L2/sidechain ("Build on Bitcoin"). Leads the [[bitcoin-sidechains]] sector; compare to the reference-winner
set in [[cycle-playbook]] (STX scores worse than HYPE/LIT/ZEC/NEAR on value accrual).

- **Narrative:** Bitcoin Staking — lock BTC (self-custodial, timelocked on Bitcoin L1) + STX worth 5% of the BTC
  value, earn ~3% annualized BTC yield via PoX. Genesis Bond launched Sep 10 2026; Bond 2 doubled it (505 BTC);
  735 BTC bonded total across the first two periods as of the Q3 2026 Ecosystem Report (stacks.co/blog, apnews.com
  press release).
- **Catalyst/distribution:** institutional custody integrations — 21Shares, HashKey Cloud, UTXO Management, Sypher
  Capital participating; custody via Fireblocks, Fordefi, Anchorage Digital (coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/stacks).
  Monthly bonding periods planned for Q4; Epoch 4.1 upgrade (Clarity 7 default) targeted early December.
- **Value accrual: none / negative.** STX has **no supply cap** and is inflating ~+5.4%/yr (1,000 STX per Bitcoin
  block to miners + 1,140 STX per miner turn to the Stacks Endowment, both raised Jul 30 2026); 0 STX burned or
  bought back in the 90 days to Oct 17 (mrnasdog.com/research/stx/inflation). Staking locks STX non-custodially but
  doesn't reduce total supply. This is the main reason STX fails the [[cycle-playbook]] checklist today.
- **Unlocks/insiders:** fully vested — "every early investor, team and token-sale allocation... is unlocked,
  circulating supply equals total supply" (mrnasdog.com). The only scheduled "unlock" is the Endowment's own
  self-release (~4.17M STX/month, already counted as circulating) — next Nov 28, ~0.22% of supply
  (tokenomist.ai/coingecko). Low near-term insider-dump risk, but permanent dilution risk instead.
- **Decentralization:** unknown/unscored. To check: validator/miner concentration, who controls the Stacks
  Endowment's spending, Muneeb Ali's move to CEO of Stacks Labs (Oct 15) and what control that role carries.
