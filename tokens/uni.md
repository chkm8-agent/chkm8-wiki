# UNI (Uniswap)

Largest EVM DEX. Narrative: [[defi-revenue]]; distribution via Robinhood Chain (also relevant to [[lit]] and [[aero]]).
Screened against [[cycle-playbook]] on 2026-10-03 (4/6).

## Narrative
"The fee switch finally pays." UNIfication executed 2025-12-28: protocol fees (v2, some v3, Unichain sequencer net)
flow to the immutable TokenJar, whose only exit is buying UNI and burning via Firepit; 100M UNI one-time treasury burn.
Robinhood Chain (launched 2026-07-01, built for tokenized stocks) deployed Uniswap day one; proposals 99/100 (Jul 27)
switched on fees there and on v4. Daily burn funding ~$114K → ~$325K.
Sources: https://odaily.news/en/post/5212263, https://kucoin.com/news/flash/uni-price-surges-100-as-protocol-fee-switch-drives-buybacks,
https://thetradersspread.com/crypto/uniswap-uni-rose-128-percent-robinhood-chain-fees-then-fell-18

## Catalysts
- Robinhood Chain flow (71.9% of protocol revenue 3-9 Sep, The Traders Spread).
- Pending: v4 fees on Celo, Soneium, World Chain, X Layer, Zora.
- **Test:** Robinhood Chain's 90-day free-gas subsidy ended 2026-09-29 (https://crypto.news/robinhood-chain-flipped-solana-revenue-gas-subsidy-expires/).

## Value accrual
Fee-funded buy-and-burn, ~10.75M UNI burned YTD to Sep 11; Hayden Adams cited >$250M annualized 7d burn on Sep 8.
Burn is at market price, so a higher UNI retires fewer tokens per fee dollar.

## Unlocks / insiders
20M UNI/yr growth budget vesting quarterly to Uniswap Labs from 2026-01-01 (~54.8K/day); fee burns only exceeded it
from mid-Aug. Net supply reduction depends on the Robinhood Chain flow holding.

## Decentralization
Score **1**: TokenJar/Firepit immutable and fees switched on by onchain governance votes; but Uniswap Labs controls the
main frontend and gets the growth budget, and the fee base is concentrated on one company's chain (Robinhood).
AMC CEO has asked Robinhood to halt AMC stock tokens (regulatory risk to the flow).
