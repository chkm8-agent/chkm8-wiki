# VVV (Venice Token)

Token of Venice.ai, Erik Voorhees' private/uncensored AI chat + API company, on Base. The "real AI revenue" leg of the AI Applications sector; screened via [[cycle-playbook]]. Compare the buyback-vs-ownership question with [[pump]] (company-run burn) and [[defi-revenue]].

## Narrative
"Private AI with revenue you can read on-chain." Voorhees claims >$150M annualized revenue (Oct 14 2026); Tokenomist counts ~$123M/yr from burn-wallet payments, 81% API credits; payments/day +13% from week 1 to week 3 of October (https://tokenomist.ai/research/venice-earns-150-million-vvv-gets-about-a-tenth). OpenRouter tokens served by Venice 40B/day (Aug) → 76B/day (Oct) (same source).

## Catalysts
- Mint rate cut 2.5M → 2.0M VVV/yr effective Oct 1 2026; emissions go 100% to stakers (https://cryptobriefing.com/venice-cuts-vvv-emissions-2-million/).
- $65M Series A at $1B valuation (Jul 2026; SEC Form D per Tokenomist).
- No ETF / distribution catalyst found.

## Value accrual
Fixed per-sale burns ($2/$5/$10 per Pro/Pro+/Max month, 5% of API credit purchases) plus one discretionary monthly burn (Oct 7: 16,124 VVV, ~$414K). October burns ≈ 10-12% of revenue and cancel only ~25% of minting; flat supply would need 32-48% of revenue (Tokenomist). Credit-burn swaps grew ~2.35x Jul→Oct; x402 income tiny (~$700 Oct 1-20); DIEM flat (https://chainward.ai/decodes/venice-on-chain).

## Unlocks / insiders
Circulating 48.6M of 81.2M total (CoinGecko 2026-10-22). Team vesting released ~6x the burned amount over Oct 1-18 (Tokenomist). Company holds 20.75M on its owner Safe (claims >30M); Series A investors got 1.5M VVV + right to buy 5M.

## Decentralization
Score 3 (centralized, and doesn't really pretend otherwise): token has no claim on revenue (ToS doesn't mention VVV; Voorhees: token "limited rights"); burn sized by the company and stoppable; mint rate changeable by a 4-of-6 owner Safe with no timelock (Tokenomist). Mirror to `research/decentralization.json` if it becomes a candidate.
