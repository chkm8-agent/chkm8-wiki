# WAL (Walrus)

Decentralized blob storage on Sui, built by Mysten Labs (the Sui team). Pitched as "the data platform for AI":
store, access-control and prove integrity of large files. Narratives: AI data provenance, see also [[cycle-playbook]], [[l2-shutdowns]] (DATA retiring its own chain is another "single-use chain too expensive" case).

DATA retires its own L1, moves to Sui, and Walrus becomes the default storage layer for DATA Foundation apps
(Trace registry: 250M+ contributions, 3M+ receipts/day). Announced Oct 12, 2026.
