# ZK: ZKsync

**One-liner:** "Real-time Ethereum proving on one GPU", plus a private-ledger product (Prividium) for banks. Narrative: [[l2-shutdowns]] (the L2/ZK squeeze leg, alongside [[strk]]). Screened against [[cycle-playbook]].

## Narrative / catalysts
- **Airbender Prime (Oct 13 2026):** proved 99% of 7,200 Ethereum mainnet blocks in under 10 seconds (all within 12 seconds) on a single RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, and tops the ETHproofs.org leaderboard. Vitalik praised it on Oct 15, then asked for formal verification and bounds on prover cost (https://cryptobriefing.com/zksync-airbender-real-time-prover-vitalik/).
- **NexBridge MoU (Oct 5-7):** NexBridge will distribute Prividium to Japanese banks, trust banks and securities firms. There are no deployments, committed volumes or revenue share (https://asksurf.ai/pulse/en/zk-japan-hype-lacks-token-capture). Surf's verdict was "fade."
- **v31 native interop (May 2026):** interop fees between ZKsync chains are denominated in ZK (a fixed 10 ZK per call per CMC). This is the only fee-capture hook so far, and we have no data on interop fee volume (https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/zksync/latest-updates/).
- A buyback was *proposed* by the founder (cryptorank). Whether it's live is unverified.

## Unlocks / insiders
- About 10.82B of 21B (51.5%) is circulating (Surf, citing CoinGecko).
- Governance draft: **67M ZK a month to Matter Labs for 12 months** (~$1M/month at the time) (https://forum.zknation.io/t/tpp-draft-2026-zksync-protocol-network-development-allocation/1005). Whether it passed is unverified.

## Decentralization
2/3: Matter Labs builds and steers the project, and a Security Council can upgrade the protocol (it did in Aug 2025 for an undisclosed issue). The flagship enterprise product is a permissioned private ledger.
