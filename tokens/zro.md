# ZRO (LayerZero)

An interoperability protocol (cross-chain messaging) that is expanding into its own chain (Zero) and an exchange backend (ATLAS).
Narrative: [[defi-revenue]] (revenue → buyback/burn), screened against [[cycle-playbook]]. Its buyback-vs-unlock tension mirrors [[lit]] and the 10-05 ENA unlock.

## Narrative
"Cross-chain rails turned into an exchange backend that burns ZRO." ATLAS is a headless exchange engine (matching, clearing, settlement, risk) on the Zero blockchain,
expected to launch "later this year" (The Block, 2026-08-25: theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-08-25-zro-token-layerzero-atlas-exchange-infrastructure-zero-blockchain-412736).
Zero was announced Feb 2026 with reported backing from Citadel and Google (Bit2Me news blurb, unverified).

## Catalysts
- ATLAS launch (date TBA, "later this year"). Venues stake up to 1% of ZRO supply for top rebate tiers (layerzero.network/blog/introducing-atlas).
- Zero mainnet. ZRO becomes the DPoS security and gas token.

## Value accrual
- ATLAS: venues get 20-65% rebates, then **25% goes to market creators and 75% to buying and burning ZRO** (layerzero.network/blog/introducing-atlas).
- Live open-market buybacks funded by Stargate revenue: 162k ZRO ($347k) on 2026-10-06, 2.538M ZRO / ~$5.4M cumulative (Arkham via gate.com/news/detail/24774007; crypto.news/can-layerzero-price-hit-3-after-zro-buybacks/).
- In Sep 2025 the Foundation bought back 50M ZRO (5%) from strategic partners. It's re-locked until Zero mainnet. Labs made a $10M discretionary buy in Nov 2025 (layerzero.network/blog/the-zro-token).
- Protocol fee switch: 3 referendums passed (>96% yes) on 10.96% / 13.01% / 3.71% turnout. We couldn't confirm the switch is active (same source).

## Unlocks / insiders
Strategic partners 32.2% and core contributors 25.5% unlock linearly, **23.08M ZRO (2.3% of supply, ~$50M) on the 20th of each month through ~Jun 2027**.
Next: **2026-10-20**. ~62% unlocked as of Sep 20, and 13.3% of supply is "untracked" (coinlaunch.space/projects/layerzero/vesting/).
Monthly unlock ≈ 9x the cumulative open-market buyback. The overhang dominates until ATLAS fees exist.

## Decentralization
**2/3.** LayerZero Labs/Foundation run the buybacks, treasury and product roadmap. Governance turnout is tiny (3.7% on the last fee vote). DVNs/executors are
independent operators per the docs. "Immutable, permissionless" messaging, but the token economics are company-directed.
